# Beta testing guide

Thanks for helping test OxyBrush! Reports from different systems and workflows help us find regressions. Twenty minutes of trying it
and one honest report is a real contribution.

## 1. Install

Download from the [latest release](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/releases/latest).

**macOS 11 or newer (Apple Silicon or Intel)**
- Open `OxyBrush-…-macos-universal.dmg` and drag **OxyBrush** onto **Applications**.
- The app isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch is blocked. Open it once, then go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click the app in Applications and choose **Open**.
- On macOS, use **Cmd** wherever this guide says Ctrl, and **Option** for Alt.

**Windows 10/11 (64-bit)**
- Run `OxyBrush-…-windows-x86_64-setup.exe`. It installs for your user only and doesn't need admin rights.
- Windows will warn that the installer is from an unknown publisher, because it isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- Prefer no installer? Unzip `…-windows-x86_64-portable.zip` and run `oxybrush.exe`.

**Linux**
- Flatpak (recommended):
  ```bash
  flatpak install --user OxyBrush-4.0.0-beta.18-linux-x86_64.flatpak
  flatpak run io.github.crisprintsstuff.OxyBrush
  ```
  The first install downloads the freedesktop runtime from Flathub, so you need the Flathub remote (`flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo`).
- Or unpack `…-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and run `./oxybrush`. Needs a Vulkan or OpenGL driver and glibc 2.35 or newer.

Optional: check your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt` on Linux, `Get-FileHash` on Windows).

## 2. First ten minutes

1. **Welcome window.** Click **Sphere** (or **Start sculpting**). Pick a **Layout** there too; on a touchscreen or tablet, try **Touch friendly**. The window only appears on the first start; reopen it from **Help > Welcome screen**, and switch layouts any time in **Settings > Layout**. Try **Settings > Arrange panels** (e.g. brushes at the bottom) and drag a brush in the shelf to a new position. Tell us if anything in it is confusing. If you'd like to hear about new test builds, turn on **Settings > Updates > Tell me about new versions** (it's off by default; only GitHub is contacted).
2. **Look around.** Drag on empty space to orbit (hold **Shift** for 45° steps), scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Press **F** to frame the model, **F1** for all shortcuts. **Settings > Keymap** rebinds any key or the mouse (the **MMB orbit** preset, and **Ctrl carves, Alt masks**). Try **View > Focal length** (or the lens button beside the viewport): the model should stay about the same size while the perspective changes, and orthographic view (O) should ignore it. Try **View > Flat shading** and **View > Align cursor to surface** (the brush ring then lies on the clay; until you turn that on, and whenever the pointer is off the model, the ring stays flat to the screen). Load a matcap image of your own from the material picker (top left). **View > Background** (also under Settings) switches the viewport between the soft gradient and one flat color; Colors… sets those colors, and Reset to default puts the original gradient back. If you prefer starting strokes beside the model, turn on **Settings > Strokes can start on empty space** and orbit with the middle mouse button.
3. **Sculpt.** Drag on the sphere. Try brushes **1–9** and **0** (see the left shelf). Hold **Shift** to smooth, **Ctrl** to mask, **Alt** to invert (or switch **Raise / Carve** in the size/strength bar). Resize the brush with **[** and **]**. Click the brush, alpha or material preview at the top left to pick from thumbnails, and try the axis gizmo (top right of the viewport) and the drag buttons in the strip beside it. The ball-shaped button in that strip switches **shading** (ambient occlusion and cavity) on and off; tell us if it looks wrong or slows your computer down.
4. **Undo** with **Ctrl+Z**, redo with **Ctrl+Shift+Z**. Press **Ctrl+K** and type a few letters of a command (try "top view" or "remesh"), or press **Space** and type part of a brush name; Enter runs it. **F2** opens the built-in guide; search it for anything you got stuck on and tell us what was missing. Right-click the model (or hold **Q**) for a quick size/strength menu at the cursor. With **Grab** (key 0), hold **Alt** to pull the surface along its normal, and try **Grab through** in the brush settings.
5. **More resolution:** press **Ctrl+D** to add a subdivision level (a few times), sculpt some fine detail, then press **Shift+D** to go down a level and make a broad change; press **D** to go back up and check that the fine detail followed. **Remesh** in the right panel rebuilds even topology (it removes the levels).
6. **Color and posing:** pick the **Paint** brush (last in the shelf), choose a color and paint; try **Fill cavities** with a dark color. Switch the material to **Pearl** to see true colors. Then mask the lower half (Ctrl+drag on empty space), press **T** and drag the handles: only the unmasked half moves. Press **T** again for rotate and scale; **Esc** during a drag cancels it.
7. **Pieces:** in the right panel, **+ Add > Sphere** adds a piece. Sculpt one, Alt+click the other to switch, then **Merge** to fuse them.
8. **Save** with **Ctrl+S** (a `.oxb` project), and **Export** with **Ctrl+E** (glTF/GLB, OBJ, STL or PLY). Try a `.glb` in another 3D tool or game engine: each piece should arrive as its own object. In the **Pieces** menu, try **Make LOD chain** and **Split into loose parts** (on a model made of separate parts, e.g. an imported OBJ).
9. **Brush presets:** in the right panel, click **Presets…**, try a starter preset, then save your own brush and right-click it > **Copy brush code**. Paste that code into the **Paste a brush code** field on another computer (or after deleting the preset) to get the brush back.
10. **Alphas and selection:** pick an alpha (right panel > Alpha, e.g. Scales), set Stroke mode to **Stamp** and drag on the model. Try the **Stabilizer** slider for smooth lines. **Ctrl+drag** on empty space to mask a rectangle (press **L** for lasso), **Ctrl+Shift+drag** to show only part of the model.

## Beta.24.2 smoke test

1. **OxySpheres.** Make a mesh from a sphere with a thin branch. Edit the
   spheres and make the mesh again. The original generated piece should
   update. Sculpt on that piece, then make the mesh again. The sculpted
   piece should stay and a new piece should appear. Save, reopen, and check
   that a subsequent update still targets the latest generated piece.
2. **Poly Edit Knife.** Close a square cut entirely inside one face of a
   cube. Look for a new inner face surrounded by a polygon rim. Undo once
   to restore the original face and redo once to restore the cut.
3. **Lighter inactive pieces.** Enable it in Preferences > General. With
   two visible pieces at higher subdivision levels, sculpt on one. The
   other should draw at its lowest level during the stroke and return to
   its original display as soon as the stroke ends. Save and reopen to
   check that both pieces retained their levels.

## 3. What we most want to know

Each of these is something we couldn't check without you:

- **Does it start?** If it fails, which GPU and driver do you have? (Help > Copy system info.)
- **Pen tablets:** does pressure change brush strength (it's on by default), and does pressure → radius work when enabled? Tell us your tablet model, driver and OS (Windows Ink on or off, OpenTabletDriver, Wayland or X11).
- **Performance:** the status bar shows the triangle count and milliseconds per frame. At what triangle count does sculpting stop feeling smooth on your machine?
- **Crash recovery:** sculpt something, wait 2 minutes, then force-close OxyBrush (Task Manager / `kill`). Does it offer to recover your work on the next start?
- **Installers:** do the Start-menu entry, uninstaller and `.oxb` double-click work on Windows? Does the Flatpak show up in your app menu with its icon? On macOS, does the app start after "Open Anyway", and does it run natively (Activity Monitor > Kind: "Apple" on Apple Silicon)?
- **macOS is brand new:** any report from a real Mac (which model, which macOS version, does the GPU work, does a tablet work) is especially valuable.
- **New in 4.0.0-beta.18:** mask part of a model, then Ctrl+click it: does the mask blur, and does Ctrl+Alt+click sharpen it? With nothing masked, does a click still paint a dab? Drag a mask box or lasso and press Alt just before letting go: is everything outside it masked? Use **Visibility > Hide masked**: are the masked faces hidden, and does Undo bring them back? In an empty scene, add a primitive or start from Empty: do the brush size slider and `[` `]` work, and does the Floor grid toggle show a grid at the origin? Arrange > Sort brushes A–Z should list the brush selector alphabetically.
- **Poly Edit gizmo pivot and axes:** in Poly Edit, pick some faces, edges or points. Does the gizmo bar's **Pivot & axes** menu align to World, View or Normal? Alt+click a point, edge or face: does the pivot snap there? Alt+drag a handle: does only the gizmo move?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.17:** start with **Empty** from the welcome window, add a primitive, save, reopen, and check that the scene stays empty after removing its last piece. Import several mesh files at once (also try dropping a batch onto the window): do the pieces arrive together, with their relative sizes intact, and does one Undo remove the batch? Turn on **Group the masked area** with Edge loop at mask border, then isolate the new group; do hidden faces stay out? On macOS, select an export format from File > Export type; on other platforms, does the chosen type appear first in the dialog, and does a typed extension still win? Also check that resizing the window no longer warps the UI, that default movement brush strength is 0.5 and Front faces only starts off, that a plain click on empty space clears a Poly Edit pick but an orbit drag doesn't, that crease brushes don't leave a ridge on the mirror plane, Kelvin Grab keeps its elasticity, and hidden faces don't join visible islands in Group by islands.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.16:** turn on Living strokes (Preferences > General), make a few strokes including a curve, a stamp and a line, capture an alpha and use it, then save and reopen: are those strokes still editable, with the same alpha? In Strokes…, **Edit…** a stroke (or double-click it) and change its brush, Raise or Carve, size, strength, falloff, alpha or spacing: does that stroke change, do strokes made after it stay as they were, and does Esc put it back? In **View > Shading**, is **Hard edge angle** 40° on a new start, do the long edges of a six-sided cylinder shade flat, and does 180° (shown as Off) keep every edge smooth?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.15:** press T on a sphere: can you move, turn and scale with the one gizmo without switching modes, and does each drag undo in one step? Insert a box, open Poly Edit, pick its top face and Shift+drag the gizmo's up arrow: does it extrude, and can you keep extruding? Turn on **Soft selection** and drag a point of a denser plane: do its neighbours follow smoothly? Ctrl/Cmd+click a second piece in the Pieces list and drag the gizmo: do both move, and does one Undo put both back? In a tube's Profile editor, choose D-shape and double-click a top point: does it become a sharp corner?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.14:** in View > Shading > Lights…, make light 3 an **Area** light with shadows and drag it above a model: are its shadows sharp where the model touches another piece and softer further away? Tick **Light the matcap**: does your matcap stay, with the lights' brightness and shadows on top? Insert a low-poly box (Insert Primitive): are its faces flat and even, not blotchy? Draw a tube, open **Profile** in the Tube window, pick **Star** and drag a point: does the tube follow live? Double-click a tube point: does it become a sharp corner? In Poly Edit press **2** and **3**: does the window show only edge or point actions, and do clicks pick that kind? Right-click a brush in the shelf, **Set hotkey…**, press a key: does it pick that brush? Press **Shift+Tab**: does only the settings panel hide?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.13:** insert two spheres with Insert Primitive and Mirror X, press T, put the gizmo on one (Alt+drag the pivot) and move it: does the other move mirrored? Give Draw a Gravity of 0.5 and stroke back and forth over a sphere: does it drape clearly? Try **Stroke > Stamp** with Draw at full strength: is it as strong as a short stroke, and does it grow while you drag? Pull a long horn with **Kelvin Grab**: is it smooth, with no slit along it? In **Poly Edit**, Ctrl/Cmd+click two faces and drag one; Shift+click an edge with Insert loop; try **Two loops**.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.12:** add a small piece (a cone) and a sphere, make the sphere active and in the right panel's **Instances** section copy the cone onto it: do the copies sit on the surface, follow it when you sculpt the sphere, and change when you use **Edit source** on the cone? Try **Make real**. Paint face groups with the **Paint Groups** brush (after Paint), with Mirror X on. Mask a face group (Ctrl+M) on a low-polygon cube, use **Mask > Edge loop at mask border**, then subdivide: does that edge stay crisp? Give Clay some **Gravity** and drape a fold. In Preferences, set **Units** to Imperial and run **Plugins > Measure**: does it show inches? In **Poly Edit**, extrude a face: are the edges behind it hidden?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.11:** start an orbit with the pointer on an ear or a finger: does the view turn around that spot, keeping it under the pointer, and does starting from empty space next to the model turn around the nearest part? (Preferences > **Orbit around** switches to the last stroke or the centre.) **With a pen on Windows:** does the brush circle follow the pen while it hovers above the tablet? Draw a tube (Tube brush) and try **Twist**, **Spin** and **Style: Braid** with Spread and Turn: are the strands clean, and does **Pieces > Edit tube…** bring the shape back after saving and reopening? Open a `.glb` or `.gltf` from another app, and an OBJ with UVs: export it again and check the texture still lines up in Blender. Try the **Sharp Crease** brush for a hard fold.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.10:** with two pieces, open the material picker, choose **For: *piece* only** and pick a different matcap: does only that piece change, and is it still there after saving and reopening? Turn on Living strokes, make a stroke on one side of a symmetric model with symmetry off, then **Mirror copy** it in **Strokes…** (right panel, Layers section): does the copy land in the mirror spot, and can you fade it on its own? Make a stroke with X symmetry on and **Split by side**: can you now delete just one side? Put some strokes in two groups and export to **GLB**, then import it into Blender: do the groups show up as shape keys you can dial? Remesh a posed character's feet: do the toes stay whole?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.9:** open a human base mesh (File > New) and press **Ctrl+D**: is the new level all quads (status bar), with the edge loops intact in the wireframe (Shift+W)? Untick **Smooth** under Topology > Subdivision levels and add a level on a cube: does it keep its exact shape? Import a model with many separate parts, run **Groups > Group by islands**, **Ctrl+M** one part and **Mask > Subdivide masked** (the step-by-step is in [Adding detail to one part of a model](guides/detail-on-one-part.md)): is only that part finer, with no cracks? Remesh a model with close fingers or toes: are they still apart, and does smoothing them stay clean? Try **+ Add > Disc** with an inner radius and a slice. With Living strokes on, group a few strokes, fade the group, save, close and reopen the project: are the strokes still editable?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.8:** turn on **Preferences > General > Living strokes: keep strokes editable** (or the button in **Strokes…** (right panel, Layers section)), make a few strokes, then fade one with the Strength slider, solo it and delete it: do the later strokes stay as they were? In **Tools > Poly Edit** on a low-polygon cube, try edge **Bevel** with 3 or 4 **Bevel rows**, **Bridge** two faces, **Crease** a few edges and **Smooth > Preview** 2: does the preview follow while you drag, and does **Smooth and sculpt** give the surface you saw? Start from **File > New > Human (female)** or **(male)** and from a **Quads** sphere (+ Add > Sphere). Remesh a piece whose parts overlap, and one with holes: is the result solid, with no inner walls? Try **Topology > Rebuild > Shell** on an open sheet.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.7:** add a low-polygon cube (**+ Add > Cube**, Divisions 1) and open **Tools > Poly Edit**: drag a face to extrude it, click an edge to insert a loop, drag a point onto another to weld them. Does what lights up match what changes, and does each drag undo in one step? Turn on **Brush settings > Size > On the model**, then zoom in and out: does the brush keep its size on the model? Import an OBJ or PLY with vertex colors from another app: do the colors come in? In the transform gizmo, are the live Size and Centre fields right, and does typing an exact size work? Try **Ctrl+plus / Ctrl+minus** (view zoom) and **Ctrl+W** with and without a mask.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.6:** with two or more pieces, sculpt on one, then another, then press **Ctrl+Alt+Z** on the first: does only that piece's stroke come back off, and does **Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z** put it back? Try **Preferences > General > Undo works on the active piece** so plain Ctrl+Z does it. Make a Freehand stroke, then **Edit > Last stroke > Edit as curve**: does the curve sit where you drew, can you reshape it and press Enter to apply? Also try **Use as Curve Move curve** and **Use as tube path**.
- **From 4.0.0-beta.5:** open **Tools > Deform…** and drag a few bars (Bend, Twist, Taper, Inflate, Polish) on your own model: does the preview feel smooth, and does letting go apply it as one Undo step? Mask part of the model first: does the masked part stay put? Try **Repeat on other pieces** with two or more pieces. In the **Mask** menu, try **Mask border** on an open mesh (or after hiding part of one) and **Mask group borders** on a model with face groups. Is the new interface font easier to read, and is the text too dim or about right on your screen?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.4:** in **View > Theme… > Colors**, try a **Custom button color** and **Brush icons > Match accent**: do the colors look right in every theme, and is button text still readable? sculpt one side of a model with X symmetry off, then try **Tools > Symmetrize > +X onto −X**: is the other side an exact copy, with no seam? Mask part of a piece and use **Mask > Split masked**: does the masked part become its own piece? Select two or more pieces, keep them visible and press **Join** in the Pieces panel: are they one piece now, looking exactly the same, and does one Undo bring them back? In the retopology tool, hold **Tab** and drag a border edge: does a new quad come out? Try the new bundled plugins in the **Plugins** menu (Group by Angle, Hide Faces by Angle, Color by Group, Fill Holes, Simplify Mesh).
- **From 4.0.0-beta.3:** open **View > Theme…**: can you find everything that used to be under Preferences > Appearance and View > Background? Try **Slider thickness > Thick**. **With a pen or tablet:** drag over empty space in the brush list or the right panel: does it scroll, while sliders still move as usual? Hold the pen still on a button for about a second: does its tooltip or menu appear? Try **Preferences > Keymap > Pen side buttons** and orbit or pan with the pen's side buttons. - **From 4.0.0-beta.2:** in the retopology tool, Alt+click points to pin them, then Shift+drag to relax: do the pins stay put, and are they still pinned after saving and reopening? Drag on the axis gizmo at the top right: does it orbit, and does a click on an axis end still snap? With **Show at startup** on, double-click a `.oxb`: does it open without the Home window on top? On macOS, open OxyBrush once from Applications: do `.oxb` files then show the OxyBrush icon in Finder?
- **From 4.0.0-beta.1:** start a project with **File > New > Cube** and sculpt along its edges: is it smooth now? Capture an alpha at **2048** or **4096 px** (the size picker next to **Capture from view**): are the edges smooth, and how long does it take on your model? Save the project, reopen it: is the capture still there? Try **Export alpha…**, edit the PNG in a 2D app and load it back with **Load image…**.

## 4. Reporting

- **Bugs:** in the app, **Help > Report a bug…** opens a GitHub issue form with your version, OS and GPU filled in. Please include steps to reproduce and attach the log file (**Help > Open log folder**; after a crash use `oxybrush.previous.log`).
- **Impressions:** **Help > Send feedback…** — what felt good, what was frustrating, what you missed.
- Don't attach files you don't have the right to share (and never ZBrush or other proprietary project files).

Log locations if the app won't start:

| Install | Log folder |
|---|---|
| Windows | `%LOCALAPPDATA%\OxyBrush\logs` |
| macOS | `~/Library/Application Support/oxybrush/logs` |
| Linux (tarball / source) | `~/.local/share/oxybrush/logs` |
| Linux (Flatpak) | `~/.var/app/io.github.crisprintsstuff.OxyBrush/data/oxybrush/logs` |

## 5. Known limitations

See the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md). Highlights: macOS is untested on real Macs, tablet pressure is unverified, and retopology is new and tried only on simple shapes.

## Uninstalling

- **Windows:** Settings > Apps > OxyBrush > Uninstall (or delete the portable folder).
- **macOS:** drag OxyBrush from Applications to the Trash.
- **Flatpak:** `flatpak uninstall --user io.github.crisprintsstuff.OxyBrush`
- Autosaves and logs live in the folders above; delete them if you want a clean slate.
