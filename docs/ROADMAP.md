## Completed

### Phase 0 — Foundations ✅
Workspace, CI (Windows/Linux/macOS), wgpu matcap renderer, chunked mesh core, headless screenshot test.

### Phase 1 — Sculpt core ✅ (one item open)
- ✅ Stroke engine with spacing, symmetry, per-chunk undo
- ✅ Brushes: Draw, Clay, Smooth, Inflate, Flatten, Amplify, Scene Project, Grab, Mask
- 🚧 **Pen pressure**: wired through winit touch/pen force; **not yet verified** with Windows Ink or Linux (libinput/Wayland tablet-v2) tablets. Needs alpha-tester feedback; may need a dedicated tablet input layer.

### Phase 2 — Topology and documents ✅ (first pass)
- ✅ Brushes: Clay Strips, Pinch, Crease, Layer, Snake Hook (12 total); radial symmetry
- ✅ Multiple pieces per document; voxel remesh and merge (union); quadric decimation
- ✅ Face groups, hide/isolate/delete hidden, group color overlay
- ✅ `.oxb` project format, autosave, crash recovery, unsaved-changes prompt
- ✅ Background jobs for remesh/merge/decimate; scene-wide undo

### Release packaging ✅
Inno Setup installer, Flatpak (`io.github.crisprintsstuff.OxyBrush`), macOS universal app + DMG (0.1.0), portable archives, app icon, SHA-256 checksums, release-triggered workflow. *(Originally planned for Phase 3.)*

## Known limitations (v4.2.0)

- Older OxyBrush betas may treat an empty-scene `.oxb` file as damaged because earlier readers require at least one piece.
- macOS: not notarized (first launch needs "Open Anyway"); opening `.oxb` files by double-click is new and not yet confirmed on a Mac; `.oxb` files could show a generic "?" icon when macOS missed the app's document types; OxyBrush now registers itself at every start from its installed location (since 4.0.0-beta.2), not yet confirmed by testers
- Alphas captured or loaded in a session stay in the list after closing a project and are saved with the next one too
- Import scaling guesses from size alone: an import more than 8× larger or smaller than the scene is scaled by a power of ten (one Undo keeps the original size); several files imported together share that one factor so their relative sizes stay; the box/lasso hide and show tools don't follow symmetry yet
- Last stroke: Replay stamps the most recent Freehand stroke again while its recipe and topology still match; Edit as curve / Curve Move / tube only while that stroke is still the newest change on its piece; per-piece undo can't undo the creation of pieces made by Join, Duplicate or Split (use whole-scene undo)
- Deform window: only Twist has been tried in the running app (the others by tests); axis choices aren't remembered between sessions; heavy smoothing at full strength previews at about 10 frames a second on a million triangles
- Symmetrize welds only points that lie on the middle plane: parts that don't reach the middle (two eyes, say) are mirrored but not joined, and both halves share the same face groups
- Decimation takes about a second for a million triangles (runs in the background)
- Open surfaces remesh to a thin shell; the Regular Grid engine is capped at resolution 320 (the default sparse engine goes to 1024)
- Voxel remesh Preserve volume option prevents shrinkage and loss of silhouette mass over successive remeshes
- Subdivision levels: changes above 1M triangles run in the background (the piece can't be edited or saved until they finish); remesh, decimate and single subdivide remove the levels (undo restores them)
- Saving to network drives: the fallbacks were tested only with simulated failures, not on a real SMB share
- Touch gestures (pinch, two-finger orbit and undo) and pen pressure are implemented but untested on real touchscreens and most tablets; Linux pen pressure relies on the tablet's pressure axis being labelled "Abs Pressure"
- Windows installer is not code-signed (SmartScreen warning)
- Dynamic detail rebuilds the whole piece on each refine: smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles, behind the brush around a million; Freehand strokes only; one row of thinner triangles where fine detail meets the untouched mesh
- Curve Move: changing its reach with a curve open is slow on dense meshes (100–200 ms at 1.3M triangles); only one curve at a time, and an applied curve can't be edited again
- Tube tool: points move on the view plane (no surface snapping); the tube stays a separate piece until merged; only a piece's latest tube can be edited again, and not once it's sculpted
- Blender bridge: interactive OBJ round trip verified on Blender 4.3.2 for Linux. Sends use the running receiver window in the same profile. Other platforms still need interactive verification.
- In-app plugins: writes replace a whole piece's attribute (slow on very large meshes); a preview shows only the active piece; add-ons are not signed
- Poly Edit: a rounded bevel's corner where three edges meet is one many-sided face, not a grid; Smooth and sculpt hands over the subdivided mesh, and Ctrl+D then adds (Loop) subdivision levels on top, not more Catmull–Clark ones; the edge and point highlight is drawn through the model; hovering checks every face, so it is meant for low-polygon pieces; the first change to a converted piece's faces drops its mask and vertex color
- Retopology Contour builds strips from closed cross-sections. Tab+drag extends a border by one quad. Edge sliding is still planned.
