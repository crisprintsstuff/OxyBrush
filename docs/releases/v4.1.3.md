## Modeling and topology

- **OxySpheres.** Choose **Rotate** and drag from a sphere or link to turn the branch around its parent. Use **Link** to twist along the limb. Mirrored branches turn together, and each drag is one Undo step. **Quad base mesh** can connect multiple three-way junctions along straight or bent links, with Blend, Resolution, and Subdivision available. [Branch rotation](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/629) · [Connected junctions](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/628)
- **Retopology Contour.** Drag successive cuts across a limb to build rings and bridge them with quads. Use **New strip** to start another section. [#621](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/621)
- **OxyRemesh.** The isoline extractor can form cells at interior grid crossings. OxyRemesh fits cut UV charts with integer-aligned seams and uses the result when it passes coverage and topology checks. Closed inputs require closed output. Joint integer optimization and coverage on curved sculpts are still in development. [Interior crossings](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/589) · [Fitted cut charts](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/603)
- **Rebuild lower level.** Run recovery repeatedly on compatible closed quad meshes to add coarser levels beneath the base while keeping the displayed level and fine detail. Recovery requires a base without UVs or explicit creases. Bake editable strokes before rebuilding. [#620](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/620)
- **Sculpt Layers.** Sculpt and paint layers stay editable when you switch or add subdivision levels. Record masked gizmo poses in a layer using Living strokes, and use **RGB Strength** to set **Also paint** opacity independently of sculpt strength. [Layer editing across levels](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/616) · [RGB Strength and poses](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/618)

## Brush and viewport fixes

- **Alphas and Roll.** Import JPEG, TIFF, WebP, and 8- or 16-bit grayscale or RGB PSD composites alongside PNG. Rotation updates the thumbnail live, directional alphas follow the stroke from the first dab, and Roll samples across repeat seams with steady pitch under pressure. The Alpha section stays open when you change images. Typed Roll distance commits when editing finishes, and the Curve stroke and Curve Move windows can be moved. [Controls and windows](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/604) · [Preview, Roll, and formats](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/627)
- **Scene Project Stamp.** Resizing a stamp reuses the target meshes and ray index. Large previews compute in the background while the previous preview remains visible. [Target reuse](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/614) · [Background previews](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/621)
- **Imported mesh smoothing and bottom shelf.** Smooth follows original polygon edges where available, including on imported quads and n-gons. Mask, selection, and cutter shapes fit in two vertical columns on the bottom brush shelf. [#625](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/625)
- **Presets and cutters.** Open brush preset actions by right-clicking or using the row's action button. Plane Clip, Line Split, and Cut Away follow multi-point Curve paths. Choose **Line** for a straight cut. [#617](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/617)
- **Split view and Poly Edit.** Both panes redraw and accept sculpting and navigation independently, with their own references, grid, and X-ray. Box, Lasso, and Ellipse draw a live selection outline. [#588](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/588)
- **Pen pressure and Windows startup.** Light initial pressure is preserved, and missing samples use the last measured force. The Intel UHD Graphics 600 startup policy now selects Direct3D 12 first. Confirmation on the reported mini PC is pending. [Pen pressure](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/581) · [Intel startup](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/577)

## Python and local MCP

Plugins can patch selected vertices, group consecutive geometry writes into one Undo step, identify their own geometry changes, and read the desktop lighting rig. They can display a temporary viewport image in single-viewport mode. The image clears when the camera, dimensions, or scene changes. [Sparse writes and viewport images](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/621) · [Grouped Undo, origins, and lights](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/627)

## Project files

Existing `.oxb` projects open in 4.1.3. Optional sections preserve native-level layer edits and clipped paint, and OxySpheres poses use the existing armature data. Older versions can display the saved mesh and visible color. Use 4.1.3 to edit the new native-level layer data and directional alpha stroke recipes.
