**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.23.3** adds **OxySpheres**: block out a figure or creature with a tree of spheres, then turn it into one smooth mesh to sculpt. It also makes the Poly Edit edge Bridge join edges instead of whole holes, and fixes Knife cuts that started or ended inside a face. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## OxySpheres

- **Pieces > + Add > OxySpheres…** opens one sphere in the middle of the view. Drag from a sphere to pull out a new one, **Shift+drag** to move one, **Ctrl+drag** up or down to resize it, click a link to put a sphere on it, and **Alt+click** (or **Delete**) to remove a sphere with the ones below it. Drag away from the spheres to orbit.
- **Mirror across X** (on when X symmetry is) changes both sides together.
- The viewport shows the mesh as it will be, with each sphere's outline over it. **Resolution** sets how fine the mesh is and **Blend** how round the joins are.
- **Make mesh** (Enter) adds the mesh as a new piece in one undo step. **Pieces > Edit OxySpheres…** reopens the spheres while the mesh hasn't been sculpted, remeshed or subdivided since.
- While the spheres are open, Undo and Redo step through their own edits.
- [#437](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/437), [#441](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/441), [RFC 0034](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/docs/rfcs/0034-oxyspheres.md)

## Poly Edit

- **Edge Bridge joins edges.** Click a border edge, then another: one quad joins them (**Rows** for more). Pick a row of border edges on each side and click one of each for a strip of quads. Picking two whole hole borders (Ctrl/Cmd+double-click) still makes a tube between the holes. Before, any two edges bridged the whole holes they were on. [#444](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/444)
- **The Knife always finishes a cut.** An end clicked inside a face now carries the cut on to that face's edge; before, such ends were dropped, so a cut within one face or into the next cut nothing. A click on the last point again also cuts, so a pen alone can finish. [#444](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/444)

## Project files

This release adds an optional `ARMS` section that keeps the spheres a piece was made from. Earlier versions skip it and open the piece as an ordinary mesh.
