## Changes in this release

**OpenVDB Sparse Volumetric Grid & Voxel Remesher (`oxy-vdb`)**
- Built-in hierarchical sparse VDB volumetric B-tree implementation in safe, pure Rust, based on the Academy Software Foundation (ASWF) OpenVDB architecture.
- $8^3 = 512$ voxel leaf nodes with 512-bit active value masks (`BitMask512`) and linear spatial index-to-world transforms.
- Lifts the previous $320^3$ resolution wall; sculptors can now remesh with resolution targets up to 1024 or 2048 with low memory consumption and fast multi-threaded execution.
- Narrow-band SDF generation with normal-oriented winding and topological exterior flood-fill, ensuring watertight manifold geometry without dense 3D grid bloat.
- **Adaptivity Slider:** Easily simplify flat planar surfaces while preserving high-density topology across curved and sculpted features.
- Dual contouring surface extraction with built-in surface relaxation and boundary truncation protection.

**Volumetric CSG Booleans (`Mesh > Boolean (OpenVDB)`)**
- True Constructive Solid Geometry Boolean tools operating directly on volumetric level sets:
  - **Boolean Difference:** Cleanly subtract a cutter piece from the active sculpt piece.
  - **Boolean Union:** Seamlessly weld two pieces into one watertight manifold surface without internal geometry or seam cracks.
  - **Boolean Intersection:** Retain only the common overlapping volume of two pieces.
- Immune to non-manifold edges, self-intersecting polygons, and co-planar face errors.
- Runs asynchronously in worker threads with full compound undo/redo history.

**Unified Preferences Window System (`Ctrl+,` or `Edit > Preferences…`)**
- Replaced the large Settings dropdown menu with a sleek, multi-tab Preferences window.
- Dedicated tabs for **General**, **Appearance**, **Plugins**, **Keymap**, **AI Agent**, and **Updates**.

**Top Menu Bar Reorganization**
- Cleaned up top menu bar into 8 clear, standard categories: `File`, `Edit`, `Pieces`, `Mesh`, `Groups`, `Mask`, `View`, `Help`.

**Grab Brush Symmetry Overlap Creasing & Boundary Tearing Fix**
- Replaced singular radial derivative blending with $C^2$ cubic directional weighting ($\omega_i = w_i^3$) and maximum envelope blending.
- Eliminates circular boundary tearing and jagged midline creases when symmetry planes overlap or strokes cross the axis.
