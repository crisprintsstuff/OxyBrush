## Highlights since 4.1.1

- **OxyRemesh Swiss cheese** ([#551](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/551), [#586](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/586)). Closed inputs are no longer replaced by open, holey extracts. Dual-graph extract densifies unit edges, collapses short dual edges / 3-cycles, fills short polygon holes, and seals remaining gaps (falling back to a closed voxel remesh when the field shell cannot enclose). Isoline extract stays gated until coverage is good; full Autoremesher-quality parameterization remains [RFC 0044](../rfcs/0044-auto-retopology.md) Phase 4.

## Project files

Projects from 4.1.1 open unchanged. No new `.oxb` sections.

## Known limitations

- macOS is not notarized (first launch needs **Open Anyway**).
- Pen pressure on Windows Ink and many Linux tablets still needs more tester confirmation.
- Dynamic detail is smoothest below a few hundred thousand triangles.
- When OxyRemesh cannot close a field extract, the result may be a closed **voxel** remesh near the face budget (not Instant Meshes quads). Full Autoremesher-quality remesh is still open ([#551](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/551)).
- Scale cover Phase 3 (fitted plates, vertex-color types, Noise underlay) remains open.
