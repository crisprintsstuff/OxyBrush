**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.21.2** fixes tabbed workflow interactions (closing tabs via `×`, opening new tabs with `+` or `Ctrl+T` from untouched tabs, and **File > New** alongside unsaved tabs) and converts the Parts Bin into a collapsible pull-out drawer anchored to the brush shelf edge. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## Fixes & Improvements ([#388](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/388))

- **Tabbed workflows repaired:**
  - **Tab close (`×`) button fixed:** excluded the close button from the tab body's click/drag hitbox (`tab_body`), preventing tab clicks from swallowing close-button presses.
  - **`+`, `Ctrl+T`, and File > New tab always add a tab:** separated explicit new-tab actions (`Editor::add_tab`) from empty-starter reuse (`Editor::new_tab`) so creating a new tab works even from a fresh untouched tab.
  - **Non-prompting File > New & clearer File menu:** **File > New** opens a new tab instead of asking to discard a modified tab, **New tab (`Ctrl+T`)** and **Close tab (`Ctrl+W`)** appear in **File**, `»` overflow and `+` stay pinned outside horizontal tab scrolling, and tab drag-reordering no longer competes with drag-to-scroll.
- **Pull-out Parts Bin drawer:**
  - Replaced the fixed bottom viewport bar with a collapsible, animated drawer anchored to whichever edge holds the brush shelf (**Left**, **Right**, or **Bottom**, clearing the bottom quick bar when both sit at the bottom).
  - Starts tucked away as a slim vector-chevron handle; click or drag inward into the viewport to slide it open or closed, and saving a new part from **Strokes** automatically opens the drawer to show it.
