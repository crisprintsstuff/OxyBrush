**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.7** brings the first steps of **Poly Edit**, a new tool for modeling low-polygon pieces. It also adds a **brush size that stays fixed on the model**, **vertex colors in imports**, and a transform gizmo that shows and takes **exact values**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Poly Edit** (**Mesh > Poly Edit (model polygons)…**, first steps). It models a low-polygon piece, such as a primitive from **+ Add**, a retopology mesh or anything up to 400,000 triangles. Point at a face, an edge or a point, and what its action would change lights up.
  - **Faces:** drag to **extrude** or **inset** them, drag to **move** them, or click to **delete** them. **Reach** spreads the action to a face loop, a face group, a flat region or the whole island.
  - **Edges:** click to **insert an edge loop** (drag to slide it first), **delete** edges or whole loops, drag a border edge to **extrude** it, or click a hole's edge to **close** it.
  - **Points:** drag a point to **move** it, and drop it on another to **weld** the two; or **delete** it.

  Mirror across X does the other side too. Each change is one undo step, and Escape cancels a drag. Bevel and bridge come next.
- **Brush size on the model** (**Brush settings > Size > On the model**, for all brushes). The brush keeps its size on the model as you zoom: zoom in and the circle grows with the model, so a stroke covers the same area at any zoom. **On screen** stays the default.
- **Vertex colors in imports.** OBJ files bring their colors, whether written after each vertex or in the `#MRGB` comment blocks some sculpting apps write. **PLY files** can now be imported too, with their colors. Nothing needs turning on: paint over the colors as usual.
- **Exact values in the transform gizmo.**
  - The move/turn/scale fields update live while you drag. Type a value and press Enter to land exactly on it.
  - A second row shows the actual **size** and **centre** of what the gizmo moves, and you can type those as well.
  - **Apply** keeps the changes and **Cancel** puts them back.
- **Fill mask** (Mask menu) masks the whole visible surface.

## Changed

- **Ctrl+plus / Ctrl+minus zoom the view**, not the interface (Cmd on macOS). **Ctrl+0** frames the active piece. The interface size is set only in View > Theme > Size.
- **Ctrl+W is Group from mask:** the masked faces become a new face group. With nothing masked, it groups the visible faces as before.
- **Done keeps your gizmo changes**, as before. If you'd rather Done put back what you didn't apply, turn on **Preferences > General > Closing the gizmo puts back changes not applied**.
- **The Deform window keeps up on very large pieces:** the bar follows the pointer while the model catches up, and a quick flick now applies.
- **Invert mask** leaves hidden parts alone.

Saved keymaps are updated as they load: if you never changed Ctrl+W or F, you get the new keys; keys you chose yourself are kept.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. Poly Edit pieces with polygons of more than four corners save them in a new project section; earlier betas open those files with the larger polygons split into triangles.
