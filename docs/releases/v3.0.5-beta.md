**OxyBrush 3.0.5-beta** adds a **retopology tool**: draw a clean, low-polygon quad mesh directly on your sculpt, mirrored across X. **Voxel remesh is up to 17× faster** and **decimate about 5× faster**, and both now keep your **vertex color, mask and face groups**. The **rotate gizmo** uses the familiar rings. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

## New

- **Retopology tool** (**Mesh > Retopology (draw quads)…**): rebuild a sculpt as a clean quad mesh, drawn by hand on its surface.
  - **Click** places a point on the surface. **Drag** a point, edge or face to slide it along the surface; drop a point on another to weld them.
  - Hold **Shift** to preview the quad (green) or triangle (purple) the nearest points make; **Shift+click** makes it. **Shift+drag** relaxes the points under the cursor.
  - **Tab+drag** a border edge to pull out a new quad, **Ctrl+click** an edge to insert an edge loop, and **Ctrl+Shift+click** to delete a point or face.
  - **Mirror across X** (on when X symmetry is): everything you do on one side happens on the other. Points placed near the centre snap onto it.
  - The result is a new piece, **Retopo**. Every action is one undo step, it's saved with the project, and it exports as real quads (OBJ).

## Changed

- **Voxel remesh is much faster.** A 330k-triangle sculpt remeshes in about 0.3 s at resolution 128 (was 5.4 s) and in 2.4 s at 512 (was about 13 s), on a 4-core machine. Merge and booleans speed up too.
  - Under the Resolution slider, remesh now shows about how many triangles it will make.
- **Decimate is about 5× faster** on large meshes: 1.3M → 131k triangles takes 0.7 s (was 3.8 s).
- **Remesh, Merge, booleans and Decimate keep vertex color, mask and face groups.** Merged pieces each keep a face group of their own.
- **Rotate gizmo with rings:** a ring per axis (red X, green Y, blue Z) and an outer ring for turning around the view. The point you grab follows the pointer.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from 3.0.4-beta open unchanged. A project with a retopology mesh adds a new section, which older versions skip: they still load the Retopo piece, as a plain mesh.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet tried by testers:** everything above. The retopology tool has only been tried on simple shapes; please try it on a real sculpt and tell us what you miss.
- **Retopology:**
  - Tab+drag makes one quad at a time (no strips).
  - Edge loops can't be slid.
  - The sculpt's detail isn't projected onto the result yet.
  - Undoing the very first face also removes the points it was made from.
- The Blender bridge add-on still hasn't been run inside Blender.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet.
- Brush settings aren't remembered between sessions (save them as presets).
