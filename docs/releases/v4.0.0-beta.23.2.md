**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.23.2** completes the Poly Edit Knife and To Circle tools and adds circle, ellipse and curve selections. It also brings pressure curves for each brush, a shared alpha library, a choice of mirror axis for duplicates, and an option to show inactive pieces lighter to save memory. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## Poly Edit

- **Knife.** Turn on **Knife** in the Poly Edit window and click points on the model to draw a cut. Clicks snap to corners and edges. Press **Enter** to cut: the path follows the visible surface, adds a point on every edge it crosses and splits the faces along it. **Backspace** removes the last point, and **Esc** cancels. Click the first point again to cut a closed loop. [#436](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/436)
- **To Circle.** Round picked points, edges or face corners toward a fitted circle, by the **Amount** you set. [#432](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/432)

## Masking and visibility

- **Circle, ellipse and curve selections.** Press **L**, or use the Mask panel's Selection row, to switch shapes. A circle drags out from its centre, and an ellipse from corner to corner. Both can start on the model. For a curve, click points and press **Enter**: everything on one side of the line is masked or shown, and **Alt** takes the other side. [#438](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/438)

## Brushes

- **Pressure curves for each brush.** The Stroke section shows a strength curve and a size curve. Customize either one for the active brush, or let it follow the defaults in **Pen & tablet**. Brush presets keep these settings. [#431](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/431)
- **Alpha library.** Imported PNG alphas are available in every tab and after a restart. Projects still embed the alphas imported into them and the ones their editable strokes use. [#430](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/430)

## Pieces

- **Mirror axis.** **Mirror duplicate** and **Mirror duplicate linked** let you choose X, Y or Z. [#429](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/429)
- **Lighter inactive pieces** (Preferences > General, off by default). A piece with subdivision levels that you aren't working on is shown at a lower level, which saves memory in large scenes. Picking it brings its own level back. Export, Join, Merge, Booleans and undo always use the full level. [#428](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/428)

## Fixes

- **Sliders.** Every slider now follows the theme's style, including Seam blend, the surface instance settings, the Recipe Box move panel and the interface tint. [#434](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/434)
- **Undo after a rebuild.** Undo no longer moves the wrong points on a mesh that was rebuilt outside the history, such as a linked copy after its original was remeshed. [#428](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/428)

## Project files

This release adds an optional `LITE` section. It records the full level of a piece that was saved while shown lighter. Earlier versions skip this section and open the piece at the lighter level, with all of its subdivision levels still available. Keep a copy of your project before saving it in an earlier beta.
