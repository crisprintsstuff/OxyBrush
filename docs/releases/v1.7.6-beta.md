## Changes in this release

### Grab Brush Symmetry Seam Blending Fix
- Eliminated the harsh midline crease and cutoff line that appeared when grabbing or pulling geometry across active symmetry planes.
- Restored $C^1$ continuous polynomial smooth maximum blending across overlapping symmetry dab influences, guaranteeing natural, organic transitions without pinching or derivative kinks.

### Grab Brush Strength Slider Control
- Resolved an issue where the Grab brush strength slider was ignored and the brush always displaced vertices at 100% full displacement.
- Strength now cleanly modulates Grab displacement linearly from 0% (vertices unaffected) to 100% (full cursor displacement) for both standard screen-plane and along-normal drags.
