**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.24.2** fixes two things from beta.24 feedback: Lighter inactive pieces now lower the other pieces only while you're editing, and a loop cut with the Knife inside a face keeps a clean rim of polygons. Below is also everything that changed since beta.23 (in beta.23.2, beta.23.3, beta.24 and this release), as it works now. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New in beta.24.2

- **Lighter inactive pieces only while you edit.** With the option on (Preferences > General), the other pieces with subdivision levels are drawn at their lowest level while you sculpt or edit a piece (a stroke, Paint Groups, a gizmo or Poly Edit drag), and in full again as soon as you stop. In beta.24 they stayed at their lowest level for as long as they weren't active. Only the drawing changes: nothing is rebuilt at the end of a stroke, and every piece keeps its levels for saving, export and undo. [#459](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/459)
- **Knife loops inside a face keep a polygon rim.** A closed cut drawn inside one face joins the loop to distinct corners of the face, so a six-point loop inside a quad no longer makes a fan of triangles, and concave quads made this way shade correctly. [#458](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/458)

## Everything since beta.23

### OxySpheres

- **Block out a shape with spheres.** Pieces > + Add > **OxySpheres…** opens one sphere on the symmetry plane. Drag from a sphere to pull out a new one, **Shift+drag** to move one, **Ctrl+drag** to resize it, click a link to add a sphere on it, **Alt+click** or **Delete** to remove a sphere with the ones below it. **Mirror across X** changes both sides together, and the viewport shows the mesh as it will be (**Resolution** and **Blend** set how fine and how round). [#441](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/441), [RFC 0034](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/docs/rfcs/0034-oxyspheres.md)
- **The spheres are a piece of their own, never used up.** **Make mesh** adds a new piece and keeps the spheres as an OxySpheres piece (sphere icon in the Pieces list); change them and make another mesh any time, however much you've sculpted the first. **Done** keeps them without making a mesh. Click the OxySpheres piece, or use Pieces > Edit OxySpheres…, to open it again. New spheres start on the symmetry plane, not off centre at the view's orbit point. [#453](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/453), [#449](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/449)

### Poly Edit

- **Knife.** Turn on **Knife** and click points on the model: clicks snap to corners and edges, and the cut follows the surface you see, over edges and onto the next face. Finish with **Enter**, **Cut**, or a click on the last point again (a pen alone can finish); a click on the first point cuts a closed loop. A point clicked inside a face carries the cut on to that face's edge (drawn dashed in the preview). A loop clicked entirely inside one face cuts a new face out of it, with a rim of polygons around it. [#436](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/436), [#444](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/444), [#452](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/452), [#458](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/458)
- **To Circle.** Round picked points, edges or face corners toward a fitted circle, by the **Amount** you set. [#432](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/432)
- **Edge Bridge joins edges.** Click a border edge, then another: one quad joins them (**Rows** for more). Pick a row of edges on each side for a strip of quads; pick two whole hole borders (Ctrl/Cmd+double-click) for a tube between the holes. [#444](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/444)
- **Paint the pick.** Ctrl/Cmd+drag picks every face, edge or point you pass over; started on a picked one, it takes them out. [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)

### Masking and visibility

- **Circle, ellipse and curve selections.** Press **L**, or use the Mask panel's Selection row, to switch shapes. A circle drags out from its centre and an ellipse from corner to corner; both can start on the model. [#438](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/438)
- **Curve selection as a pen path.** Drag from the first point and let go to apply, or click points and press Enter. Tap Alt while drawing for a point the line bends through, twice quickly for a sharp corner. It takes the side on your right as you draw; hold Alt as you apply for the other. [#438](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/438), [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)
- **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+click hides a face group**, the opposite of Ctrl+Shift+click. [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)

### Brushes

- **Pressure curves for each brush.** Stroke shows a strength curve and a size curve; customize either for the active brush, or let it follow Pen & tablet. Brush presets keep them. [#431](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/431)
- **Alpha library.** Imported PNG alphas are available in every tab and after a restart. [#430](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/430)
- **Paint Groups with Shift smooths jagged group borders**, sliding the border's points into a smooth line as Deform's "Polish, keep group edges" does; one undo step puts back points and groups. [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)
- **The brush ring shows while you resize from the quick menu**, as it does with S. [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)

### Pieces

- **Mirror axis.** **Mirror duplicate** and **Mirror duplicate linked** let you choose X, Y or Z. [#429](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/429)
- **Lighter inactive pieces** (Preferences > General, off by default): see New in beta.24.2. [#428](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/428), [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456), [#459](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/459)

### Fixes

- **Every slider follows the theme**, including Seam blend, the surface instance settings, the Recipe Box move panel and the interface tint. [#434](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/434)
- **Undo after a rebuild** no longer moves the wrong points on a mesh rebuilt outside the history, such as a linked copy after its original was remeshed. [#428](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/428)
- **macOS: the Plugins menu lists your plugins from the start**, not only after Settings > Plugins was opened. [#456](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/456)

## Project files

- **`ARMS`** (new since beta.23): OxySpheres. Version 2 marks an OxySpheres piece; version 1, written by beta.23.3 on a mesh made from spheres, still opens, with those spheres back as an OxySpheres piece. Versions before beta.24 open an OxySpheres piece as an ordinary mesh of the spheres.
- **`LITE`** (beta.23.2 to beta.24): a piece saved shown lighter. Such projects open with every piece at its own level; nothing is saved lighter any more.

Keep a copy of your project before saving it in an earlier beta.
