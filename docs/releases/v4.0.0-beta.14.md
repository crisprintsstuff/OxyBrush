**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.14** brings **area lights** with soft shadows, a way to **keep your matcap under the lights**, **tube profiles** (draw any cross-section) and **sharp tube corners**. **Poly Edit** now has **Faces, Edges and Points modes** (1, 2, 3), as other modeling apps do. Low-poly models shade with **crisp, flat faces**. Any brush can have its **own hotkey**, and **Shift+Tab** hides just the settings panel. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Area lights** (View > Shading > Lights…): a round light with a **Size**, facing the point you aim it at, like a soft box.
  - Its shadows are sharp where things touch and softer further away.
  - A bigger light gives softer light and wider highlights.
- **Light the matcap** (Lights window): keep your matcap as the material. The lights brighten it where they shine, and their shadows darken it.
- **Tube profiles** (Tube window > Profile): give a tube any cross-section.
  - Start from Round, Square, Triangle, Star, D-shape or Ribbon.
  - Drag the points on a small canvas: click the outline to add a point, right-click to remove one.
  - **Smooth** curves through the points; **Mirror** edits both sides at once.
  - The profile turns with Twist and shapes braids too.
- **Tube editing** ([#258](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/258)):
  - **Double-click** a tube's point for a **sharp corner**, and again for smooth.
  - Drop a point on its neighbour to merge them, or press **Delete** to remove the picked one.
  - With the Tube brush, drag away from the tube to orbit.
- **Poly Edit modes:** **1 Faces**, **2 Edges**, **3 Points**, or **4 Auto** for the old behaviour where what's under the pointer decides.
  - The window shows only that kind's actions, and clicks pick that kind, so small targets are easy to hit.
  - **Ctrl/Cmd+click** picks several edges or points too.
  - Switching modes turns the pick into the new kind: pick a face, press 2, and bevel its border.
- **A hotkey for any brush** ([#259](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/259)): right-click a brush in the shelf and choose **Set hotkey…**.
- **Hide just the settings panel** ([#260](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/260)) with **Shift+Tab** or the view strip's top button. Tab still hides every panel.

## Fixed

- **Low-poly models shade with crisp, flat faces.** A box from Insert Primitive or Poly Edit looked blotchy, with highlights smeared across its faces, under both the matcap and the lights. Spheres, cylinders and sculpts stay smooth.
- **Lights without shadows no longer end in a hard line.** Every light fades gently where the surface turns away from it, set by its **Softness**.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. This beta adds two new project sections. Older versions open files that have them as follows:

| New section | What it saves | In older versions |
|---|---|---|
| `TUBC` | Which points of a tube are sharp corners | The tube opens as ordinary geometry and can't be reopened with Edit tube |
| `TUBP` | A tube's cross-section profile | The same |

**Settings:** the viewport lights are saved in a new format (area lights and the matcap switch). Older versions don't read it and go back to their default lights.
