## Changes in this release

### 3D-Print Preparation Tools (`Mesh > 3D Print Tools...`)
- **Real-World Scale & Metrics:** Configurable scale in millimeters per world unit (`unit_scale`). Displays physical model dimensions ($W \times D \times H$ in mm), surface area ($cm^2$), and exact watertight volume ($cm^3$) computed via the divergence theorem.
- **Material Consumption Estimates:** Shows estimated material usage and weight in grams for photopolymer resin (~1.15 g/cm³) and PLA filament (~1.24 g/cm³).
- **Watertight Manifold Validation:** Checks whether the mesh is a closed 2-manifold ready for slicing.
- **Wall-Thickness Analyzer:** Parallel inward raycasting along negative normals calculates local wall thickness across all vertices. Flags fragile areas below the user-defined threshold and displays a visual warning heatmap on the sculpt (red for thin areas, orange for near-threshold).
- **Hollowing with Drain Holes (OpenVDB):** Uses narrow-band level set erosion to hollow models into uniform shells, piercing cylindrical escape holes to allow uncured resin or unsintered powder to evacuate.
- **Split into Interlocking Parts with Alignment Keys:** Cuts large sculpts across X, Y, or Z planes, creating Part A with male alignment pins and Part B with matching sockets (+0.2mm clearance tolerance) for seamless post-print assembly.

### Tablet Input & Multi-Touch Gestures
- **Continuous Stylus Pressure Pipeline:** Fixed an issue where `CursorMoved` unconditionally reset pen pressure to 1.0 during active strokes. Fully supports graphics tablet drivers (Wacom, Huion, XP-Pen, Apple Pencil, Windows Ink, Linux XInput2/Wayland) with pressure sensitivity, `TouchpadPressure`, and analog axis events.
- **Multi-Touch Gesture Layer:**
  - 1 finger: Sculpting or painting with pressure.
  - 2 fingers: Pinch-to-zoom, two-finger orbit and pan, and **two-finger tap for Undo**.
  - Trackpad gestures: Native `PinchGesture` zoom and `RotationGesture` camera roll.

### Vertical Right-Click Quick Menu
- Reorganized the quick menu at the cursor (right-click or held `Q`) from a wide horizontal bar into a sleek vertical card (175px width).
- Instant access to Raise/Carve, Size, Strength, and brush-specific controls right under your pointer without obscuring the model.

### Crease Brush Pinch Parameter
- Exposed a configurable `Pinch` slider (`0.0..=1.0`) in both the brush settings panel and the vertical quick menu.
- Controls how strongly the crease groove walls are gathered toward the crease line (0.0 = pure carving groove, 0.3 = default, 1.0 = sharp tight pinch). Saved in brush presets.

### Software Hardening & Safety Guards
- **Autosave on Suspend / Blur:** Immediate non-blocking autosave when the window loses focus (`Focused(false)`) or when suspended by the OS.
- **Corrupt File Import Guards:** Validates triangle vertex indices against vertex counts and rejects non-finite (`NaN`/`Inf`) coordinates across OBJ, STL, and PLY loaders.
- **Subdivision Memory Ceiling:** Enforces safety ceilings (`max_triangles`) using saturating multiplications to prevent integer overflow or out-of-memory crashes on dense multires meshes.
- **Topological Manifold Diagnostics:** Added `check_manifold` in `oxy-mesh::topo` detecting open boundary edges, non-manifold edges, and degenerate triangles.
