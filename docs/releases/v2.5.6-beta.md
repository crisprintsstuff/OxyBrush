**OxyBrush 2.5.6-beta** is a small update to 2.5.5: a new **Midnight** theme with **pill-shaped** controls, and documentation that matches the app again. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**First launch:**
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg`, drag OxyBrush to Applications, and open it. If macOS shows an untrusted developer warning, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **Midnight theme:** deep navy panels that read as layered glass, a thin blue outline on every button and field, softer blue-gray secondary text, and soft shadows under windows and menus.
- **Pill corners:** a new corner style (Preferences > Appearance) that gives buttons, fields and list rows fully rounded ends. It works with any theme.
- **Midnight quick preset:** one click in Preferences > Appearance applies the Midnight theme, pill corners, the emerald accent and a dark navy viewport. Your material and shortcuts stay as they are. "Theme: Midnight" is also in the command palette (Ctrl+K).

## Changed

- **Documentation:** the README, homepage, changelog and built-in guide now use the current brush, theme and preset names, and the guide explains the Windows Ink pen side button.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Known limitations

- The Midnight "glass" look is made with colors and shadows; the interface has no real blur or transparency.
- The Windows Ink pen fix from 2.5.5 hasn't been tried on every tablet yet; if the side button still sculpts or masks, please comment on [#59](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/59).
- macOS is still untested on real Macs and not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
