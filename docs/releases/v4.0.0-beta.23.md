**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.23** adds linked copies of pieces, settings and pressure curves for each brush, and smoother Boolean seams. Poly Edit can now pick whole loops and bridge holes. The release also fixes long-lens zoom, far-side wireframe edges and painted detail across subdivision levels. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## Pieces

- **Linked copies.** **Pieces > Duplicate linked** and **Mirror duplicate linked** make a copy that follows its original as you sculpt, paint or remesh it, live. Sculpt one ear and its mirror image keeps up. Move, turn or scale the copy with the transform gizmo, and it stays linked in its new place. **Unlink copy** makes it an ordinary piece. Copies can't be sculpted themselves. [#422](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/422)
- **Smoother Booleans.** The seam where two pieces meet is rounded over a few voxels instead of coming out jagged. Set **Seam blend** in the Boolean row: 2 by default, 0 for a sharp crease. The rest of the piece now keeps its shape, so earlier seams no longer soften with each Boolean. [#422](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/422)
- **Duplicate keeps subdivision levels.** Duplicate and Mirror duplicate copy every level, with its detail and paint, and the material. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)
- **Hierarchy commands.** Right-click a piece with children to **Select**, **Join** or **Merge** its hierarchy. **Join picked**, **Merge picked** and the Split commands are in the same menu. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)

## Brushes and pen

- **Settings per brush.** Each brush keeps its own falloff, focal shift, spacing, stroke mode, stabilizer, auto smooth, spray, pressure switches and **alpha**, and gets them back when you pick it again. Size stays shared unless you turn on **Size per brush**. Both options are in **Preferences > General**. [#422](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/422)
- **Two pressure curves.** **Preferences > Pen & tablet** has separate **Strength** and **Size** curves. For example, a light touch can draw a thin line at full strength. [#422](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/422)
- **Smooth group borders.** Hold Shift with the Paint Groups brush to even out jagged face group borders. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)
- **Brush ring while resizing.** The brush ring stays visible on the model while you resize with S. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)

## Poly Edit, masking and visibility

- **Loop picks.** Ctrl/Cmd+double-click picks a whole edge loop (or the whole border of a hole) or a face loop. A stray click on empty space no longer clears the pick; Esc clears it. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)
- **Bridge holes.** The new **Bridge** edge action joins two holes with a tube: click an edge of each. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)
- **Visibility and selections.** Ctrl+Shift+drag on empty space inverts visibility. Hold Space while drawing a box or lasso to move it. [#421](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/421)

## Fixes

- **Long lenses.** Zooming in no longer puts the camera inside the model at long focal lengths. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)
- **Wireframe.** The wireframe no longer shows edges from the far side of low-poly shapes. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)
- **Painted detail.** Paint survives stepping down a subdivision level and back. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)
- **Cylinders and cones.** Pieces from **Pieces > Add** keep their side quads. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)
- **Renaming a piece.** Renaming selects the name, and a click elsewhere ends it. [#420](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/420)

## Project files

This release adds two optional sections:

- **`MRPT`** keeps painted detail per subdivision level.
- **`LINK`** keeps linked copies linked.

Earlier versions skip both. They open linked copies as ordinary pieces, and they keep paint on the level that was active when you saved. Saved living strokes now use recipe version 4, which stores the pressure curves. Earlier versions can't edit strokes saved by this version, but the sculpted surface is unaffected. Keep a project copy before resaving in an earlier beta.
