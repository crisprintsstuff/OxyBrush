**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.19.2** brings a major scenes upgrade with a hierarchical tree-view outliner and transform propagation, 4K alpha imports with native 16-bit depth preservation, flexible inspector panel resizing down to 180pt with wrapping controls, and volume-preserving voxel remeshing. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New & Improved

- **Hierarchical scene tree outliner & transform propagation** ([#332](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/332), [#358](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/358)):
  - **Tree view outliner**: The Pieces panel now displays pieces in an outliner hierarchy with depth indentation and tree connector lines.
  - **Fold & unfold**: Fold child hierarchies using the arrow chevrons (`▾` / `▸`), with collapsed badge counts (`({n})`) to keep multi-piece character and prop scenes organized.
  - **Drag-and-drop parenting**: Drag a piece and drop it onto the middle of another piece to parent it. Dropping at the top or bottom edge reorders, while dropping below the list or clicking **Unparent** unparents it.
  - **Transform propagation**: Moving, rotating, or scaling a parent piece with the transform gizmo (`T`) moves all of its child and descendant pieces together around the parent's pivot (acting like an anatomical joint or limb). Transforming an individual child piece leaves its parent intact.
  - **Project format & history**: Parent relationships are saved in `.oxb` projects via the skippable `PARN` chunk (fully backward-compatible with earlier OxyBrush versions), and parenting/unparenting operations support full scene undo/redo.
- **4K alpha imports & 16-bit depth preservation** ([#359](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/359), [#361](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/361)):
  - **4096px resolution**: Raised the alpha import clamp from 1024 to 4096, unlocking crisp, high-detail stamping on dense multiresolution sculpts (e.g. 40M+ quads).
  - **Native 16-bit grayscale**: Full 16-bit depth (`u16`) is preserved on 16-bit PNG imports without quantization banding or downsampling steps.
  - **Aspect ratio preservation**: Non-square images are centered with black padding to maintain exact proportions without distortion.
  - **Area-box downsampling**: Preserves 1px ink strokes and fine lines when scaling images down.
  - **VRAM optimization**: Inspector UI previews are downsampled to 128x128 thumbnails, reducing GPU memory from 16 MB to 16 KB per 4K alpha.
- **Narrow inspector panel resizing** ([#360](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/360), [#362](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/362)):
  - The right-side inspector panel can now be dragged narrower down to 180pt (`min_size(180.0)`) to maximize 3D viewport canvas space.
  - Buttons and controls across the Alpha, Brush, Symmetry, Topology, and Groups sections now wrap and stack cleanly (`horizontal_wrapped`) rather than locking the panel to wide minimum widths.
  - Inactive piece name buttons truncate smoothly, and piece rename fields dynamically scale down.
- **Volume preservation in voxel remesh** ([#354](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/354), [#357](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/357)):
  - Optional **Preserve volume** mode projects vertices back to the level-set zero crossing and applies volume-calibrated normal adjustments to prevent shrinkage and chord loss during voxelization and smoothing.
  - Successive remeshes retain their silhouette, mass, and fine features without receding. Also available via the CLI (`--preserve-volume`).
- **Deform window title bar flick fix** ([#351](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/351), [#355](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/355)):
  - Dragging the Deform window by its title bar no longer accidentally triggers deformer sliders scrolled out of view.
- **New banner branding**:
  - The README banner has been updated with the custom project graphic.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.
