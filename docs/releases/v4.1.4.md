## OxySpheres quad hands and feet

Use **Quad base mesh** to connect an incoming forearm or ankle and up to five fingers or toes to one joint. Each link gets its own loop when the branches have enough room. Adjust crowded branches or switch to **Voxel skin** if the quad build reports an unsupported arrangement. [#635](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/635)

## Inspect and repair imports

When an import has findings, choose **Inspect import** in the status bar. The report lists open or non-manifold edges, inconsistent winding, duplicate triangles, and zero-area faces by piece. **Frame piece** brings the affected piece into view. **Repair faces** removes duplicate and zero-area triangles and fixes winding where possible. **Close holes** fills suitable open boundaries as a separate action so intentional openings can stay open. Each repair refreshes the report and can be undone in one step. Self-intersections and solid printability require further checks. [#637](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/637)
