**OxyBrush 0.4.0-alpha** is the biggest update yet: **paint color** onto your sculpts, **pose** them with a mask-aware transform gizmo, save and share **brush presets**, and export to **glTF/GLB** for game engines and other 3D tools. It also brings a **command palette**, a **built-in guide**, and several things testers asked for in the [discussions](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/discussions): flat shading, custom matcaps, a quick menu at the cursor and more. It's still an alpha: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

## New in this release

**Sculpting and painting**
- **Vertex color:** a new **Paint** brush (last in the shelf; Alt erases), plus **Fill**, **Fill cavities** and **Fill peaks** that respect the mask: great for dirt in the folds or worn edges. Saved in projects and carried through subdivision levels.
- **Transform gizmo and posing (T):** move, rotate or scale a piece. Masked parts stay put and a soft mask edge bends smoothly, so you can mask the body and rotate an arm at the shoulder.
- **Quick menu at the cursor:** right-click the model or hold **Q** for size, strength and Raise/Carve right where you work.
- **Grab** can now reach **through** the model (everything under the brush circle), and **Alt** with Grab or Snake Hook moves the surface **along its normal**.
- **Brush presets:** save your brush settings under a name, start from built-in presets, and share a brush as a one-line code or a file.

**Viewing**
- **Flat shading** (View menu), **custom matcaps** (load a PNG or JPEG from the material picker), and **45° view steps** while holding Shift and orbiting.

**Workflow**
- **Command palette (Ctrl+K):** type a few letters of any command, brush, recent file or guide topic. **Space** finds a brush by name.
- **Built-in guide (F2):** short, searchable help on every part of OxyBrush, available offline.
- **Recent files** in File > Open recent and on the welcome window.
- **Strokes from empty space** (Settings, off by default): start a stroke beside the model; orbit with the middle mouse button instead.
- **Middle-drag scrolls the side panels**, handy with a pen side button.
- **Optional update checks** (off by default; Settings > Updates): only GitHub is contacted, and downloads are checksum-verified.

**Import, export and pipeline**
- **Export to glTF/GLB** (one object per piece), **OBJ** (one object per piece), **STL** and **PLY**. Hidden faces are never exported.
- **OBJ import keeps objects apart:** each object becomes its own piece.
- **Make LOD chain** (1/2, 1/4, 1/8 of the triangles) and **split a piece** by face groups or into loose parts (Pieces menu).
- **`oxybrush-cli`**, a command-line tool for batch conversion, remeshing, decimation, LODs and splitting (in the portable downloads).

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details. Thank you to everyone who shared ideas in the discussions.

## Known limitations

- Vertex color isn't included in exports yet, and remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
- Hotkeys and mouse navigation can't be customised yet; that's planned for the next release.
- The transform gizmo uses world axes only (no numeric input or snapping yet).
- New input features (quick menu, middle-drag scrolling, grab through) haven't been tried on real tablets or pen side buttons yet; please tell us how they behave.
- macOS is still untested on real Macs; pen pressure is still unverified on real tablets.
- The Touch layout has no multi-touch gestures yet (pinch zoom, two-finger orbit).
- A `.flatpak` bundle doesn't update itself: install the newer bundle to update.
