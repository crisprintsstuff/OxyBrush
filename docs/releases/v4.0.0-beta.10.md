**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.10** lets each piece have its **own material**, completes **Living strokes** with Mirror copy, Split by side and groups exported as shape keys, opens living strokes to **plugins and agents**, and fixes the voxel remesh **tearing the toes of posed characters**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **A material per piece** (material picker and View > Material: **For: *piece* only**). Give the active piece its own matcap, built-in or one you loaded, to tell parts apart: a jade eye, a bronze armour plate. Hovering previews it on that piece only, and **Use the scene's material** puts it back. Up to 8 different piece materials show at once.
- **Living strokes, third phase** (Mesh > Strokes…, with Living strokes on in Preferences > General):
  - **Mirror copy** puts the selected strokes on the other side of the piece's symmetry plane (X, Y or Z), as new strokes you can fade on their own. It works where the piece is symmetric.
  - **Split by side** turns a stroke made with symmetry on into one stroke per side, so you can fade or delete just one side. Nothing on the model moves.
  - **glTF/GLB export** writes each stroke group as a **shape key** named after it, so the groups become sliders in Blender or a game engine. The file still looks like the viewport.
- **Living strokes for plugins and agents.** Plugins (API 4) can read the strokes and groups and run any Strokes window action, for example fading every stroke in a group. The agent (MCP) gets `list_strokes` and `edit_strokes`. See the [plugin API reference](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/docs/plugins/api-reference.md).

## Fixed

- **Voxel remesh of posed or rigged models.** Toes no longer come out hollow and torn. Bending toes down in a rig can fold the skin under them back through the sole, and the remesh read those folds the wrong way. Meshes whose normals all face inward (some exports) also remesh correctly now. Very sharp folds can still leave a small tunnel.
- The material picker stays open until you click outside it.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. This beta adds one new project section, and older versions open files that have it as follows:

| New section | What it saves | In older versions |
|---|---|---|
| `MATL` | A piece's own material | Show the scene's material |

Strokes made with Mirror copy or Split by side are saved in the existing `STRK` section, so beta.9 opens them as editable strokes too.
