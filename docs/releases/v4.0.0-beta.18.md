**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.18** adds quicker mask editing, a way to hide masked faces, and a sorted brush list, and fixes the brush size and floor grid in an empty scene. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Blur and sharpen the mask with a click.** Ctrl+click on the model blurs the whole mask and Ctrl+Alt+click sharpens it; dragging still paints or unmasks. With nothing masked, a click still paints a dab.
- **Hide masked** ([#329](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/329)) in the Visibility menu and the command palette hides the masked faces.
- **Mask outside a box or lasso** ([#329](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/329)): press Alt just before letting go of a mask box or lasso to mask everything *outside* it.
- **Sort brushes A–Z** ([#304](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/304)) in the Arrange menu lists the brush selector alphabetically.

## Fixed

- **Empty scenes** no longer pin the brush size to its minimum ([#328](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/328)): the size range follows the camera distance until there is something to measure, so the slider and `[` `]` work.
- The **Floor grid** toggle works in an empty scene; the grid is drawn at the origin, sized to the view.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

No change to the `.oxb` format: projects from earlier betas open as before.
