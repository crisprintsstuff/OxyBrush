**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.13** is mostly about brushes and tools behaving the way testers expected. The **transform gizmo follows Mirror X/Y/Z**. **Gravity** is much stronger. **Stamp** strokes are as strong as freehand and show while you drag. **Kelvin Grab** no longer creases long pulls. **Poly Edit** can pick several faces and cut centred or paired edge loops. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Poly Edit: pick several faces** ([#243](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/243)). **Ctrl/Cmd+click** faces to pick them (outlined in green). Dragging or clicking one of them then extrudes, insets, bevels, moves or deletes them all together. Ctrl/Cmd+click empty space, or **Clear**, unpicks them.
- **Poly Edit: centred and paired edge loops** ([#243](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/243)):
  - **Shift+click** an edge with Insert loop to put the loop exactly in the middle.
  - **Two loops** cuts a pair the same distance in from each end; dragging moves them in or out together.

## Changed

- **Gravity is much stronger**: about 3.3 times. At 1, one pass sags about a brush radius, so a few passes drape a whole area into a lip. 0.1–0.3 gives the gentle sag the old range had. Brushes and presets you saved keep their Gravity numbers, so they sag more than before.
- **Stamp strokes are as strong as a short freehand stroke**, instead of a faint dimple. The stamp **shows and grows while you drag**, Escape takes it back, and a click without dragging stamps at the brush size.

## Fixed

- **The transform gizmo follows Mirror X/Y/Z.** With two shapes inserted with Mirror X and the gizmo on one of them, the other side now gets the mirrored move, turn or scale, as in other sculpting apps. A part across the mirror plane stretches symmetrically. A whole model, or a pivot on the plane, still moves as one.
- **Kelvin Grab no longer creases long pulls.** Horns and ears pulled out with it had a slit from tip to base; they now come out as smooth as with Grab, and small moves keep the rubbery feel.
- **Poly Edit uses face groups painted since it was last open** ([#249](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/249)), so **Reach: Face group** picks the group you just painted.
