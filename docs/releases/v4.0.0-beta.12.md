**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.12** adds **surface instances** (copy a rivet, a spike or a scale onto every face of another piece, as live copies that follow the surface), a **Paint Groups brush**, **edge loops at the mask border** for crisp hard-surface edges, and per-brush **Gravity**. It also adds a **Units** preference, **File > Revert**, a **Layers** section and a **Tools** menu, and cleaner **Poly Edit** display. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Surface instances** (the right panel's new **Instances** section): copy one piece onto every face or point of another, or onto one face group, as live copies.
  - They sit on the surface and follow it as you sculpt.
  - **Edit source** shows the copied piece alone to sculpt, paint or reshape it, and every copy follows.
  - Choose which way the copies face (a world direction, so neighbours never flip; the face's longest or first edge; or random), their size, lift and turn, and add random size and turn.
  - **Make real** turns them into an ordinary piece and keeps the copies, so you can change them and make them real again.
  - Exports don't include the copies yet: Make real first.
- **Paint Groups brush** (after Paint in the brush shelf): paint face groups straight onto the model. Each stroke makes a new group; **Alt**+stroke grows the group you start on. It follows symmetry and never moves the surface.
- **Edge loop at mask border** (Mask and Mesh menus): adds support loops along the edge of the mask, on one or both sides and at a width you choose, so a hard edge stays crisp when you subdivide. Mask a face group (Ctrl+M) to put the loop exactly around it.
- **Gravity, per brush** (brush settings): the surface sags downward as you sculpt, for drapery and hanging folds. Each brush keeps its own amount.
- **Units** (Preferences): **Metric** or **Imperial**, and how many millimetres one scene unit is. The **Measure** plugin now shows sizes in mm (volume in cm³) or inches (in³) ([#234](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/234)).
- **File > Revert** reloads the project as you last saved it ([#218](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/218)).
- **View > Inactive pieces:** grey out (as before), darken, tint with a color you pick, or off, to see every piece in its own material.
- **View > Shading > Show vertex color** hides colors to judge the form in plain clay, and shows them again.
- **A Layers section** in the right panel for sculpt layers while you work, and a **Tools** menu for the tools that shape a piece (Deform, Transform, Symmetrize, Poly Edit, Retopology, Vertex color).
- **Booleans in the Pieces panel**: Subtract, Add and Overlap with the piece you pick.
- **Quad and UV spheres** in the Insert Primitive brush.

## Fixed

- **Poly Edit: edges behind the model no longer show through**, and **polygons with more than four corners** show as one face instead of the lines they're split into.
- **Painted areas no longer have a bright outline** at the edge of a stroke.
- **The gizmo pivot stays put** after Insert Primitive when "Gizmo pivot follows the mask" is off.
- **3D print:** the wall check can be undone, and its threshold no longer changes Hollow's wall thickness.
- **Topology diagnostics** no longer slows the app while its window is open.
- **Touch layout:** the size and strength bar no longer covers the axis gizmo.
- An **interface consistency pass**: the 3D print and diagnostics windows match the rest of the app, and menus use the same wording everywhere.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. This beta adds one new project section. Older versions open files that have it as follows:

| New section | What it saves | In older versions |
|---|---|---|
| `INST` | Surface instances: which piece is copied onto which, and how | The host and the copied piece open as ordinary pieces, the copied one hidden; the copies don't show |

**Plugins:** the in-app plugin API is now version 5. It adds `units()` and `unit_scale()`; older plugins keep working.
