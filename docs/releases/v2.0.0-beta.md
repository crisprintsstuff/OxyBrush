## Changes in this release

### Insert Primitives Brush (IMM / Multi-Mesh)
- **Drag-to-Scale & Orient:** Click anywhere on the mesh surface to anchor, and drag outward to dynamically scale and orient primitives (Sphere, Cube, Cylinder, Cone, Torus, Plane) along the surface normal.
- **Realtime 3D Wireframe Overlay:** Live interactive preview renders the primitive expanding from the anchor point with support for active radial and mirror symmetry axes.
- **Auto-Masking Pre-Existing Geometry:** When committed on pointer release, all pre-existing vertices are automatically 100% masked (`mask = 1.0`), while the newly inserted shape remains unmasked (`mask = 0.0`), allowing immediate posing, scaling, and transposition using the Gizmo (`W`).
- **Polygroup Assignment & Undo Support:** Every inserted primitive receives a distinct polygroup face group ID and records a full topology step in undo history (Ctrl+Z restores the prior geometry and masks).

### ZBrush Navigation, Keymap & Theme Presets
- **ZBrush Classic UI Theme:** A custom dark slate theme preset accurately recreating the iconic ZBrush interface look and feel, selectable in Preferences > Theme.
- **Maxon ZBrush Keymap Profile:** Authentic navigation bindings matching the official ZBrush cheat sheet, including Right-click and Alt-click viewport navigation, brush palettes, and masking shortcuts.

### Tablet Detection & Linux / Wayland Fixes (Issue #55)
- **Automatic Hardware Sensing:** Probes `/proc/bus/input/devices` and `/sys/class/input` to detect drawing tablets and styluses.
- **Automatic X11/XWayland Fallback:** Automatically switches to the X11 backend when running under Linux with a tablet and `DISPLAY` present, bypassing Wayland driver drops and ensuring sub-pixel drawing accuracy with continuous pressure streaming.
- **CLI & Environment Overrides:** Added `--x11`, `--backend <x11|wayland>`, and `OXY_BACKEND=x11`.

### 3D Print Tools Overhaul (Issue #56)
- **Signed Distance Pseudonormals:** Fixed OpenVDB mesh-to-volume sign calculation using angle-weighted vertex pseudonormal interpolation, preventing false-negative exterior signing across sharp edges and corners.
- **Dynamic Narrow Banding:** Calculated narrow band leaf boundaries dynamically based on wall thickness ($\max(4.0, \lceil \text{wall}/h \rceil + 4.0)$), eliminating surface leakage on thick hollow shells.
- **Watertight Analytic Splits:** Replaced 900MB voxel grid split operations with exact analytic half-space signed distance field intersections, delivering instantaneous (<0.1s), 100% watertight plane cuts with keyed socket/pin assemblies.
