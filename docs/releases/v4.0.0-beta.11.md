**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.11** lets you **orbit around the point under the cursor**, adds **twisted, coiled and braided tubes**, opens **glTF/GLB files**, exports them **compressed**, keeps **UVs** from import to export, and adds the **Sharp Crease** brush and **sculpt layers you make ahead of time**. It also fixes the brush cursor not following a **hovering pen on Windows**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Orbit around the cursor** (Preferences > **Orbit around**). Orbiting now turns around the point of the model under the pointer where you start it. That point stays put on screen, so you can turn around any spot just by pointing at it. Starting from empty space turns around the nearest part of the model. You can choose **Last stroke** (where your last stroke ended) or **Centre of the view** (as before) instead. **F** goes back to the centre until you orbit or sculpt again.
- **Tube Twist, Spin and Braid** (Tube window):
  - **Twist** turns the tube along its length.
  - **Spin** coils it around its path like a spring, with a coil size and number of turns.
  - **Braid** weaves three round strands, from tight to loose (**Spread**), with as many weaves as you like (**Turn**). The strands never pass through each other.
  - They combine, and **Edit tube…** reopens them.
- **glTF/GLB import.** Open `.glb` and `.gltf` files (File > Open, Import, or drag and drop). Each mesh becomes a piece, placed where its scene puts it, with vertex colors. Files compressed with meshopt or quantization open too. Draco-compressed files can't be opened yet, and OxyBrush says so.
- **Compressed glTF/GLB export** (File > **glTF compression**): **Quantized**, or **Meshopt** for files about a quarter of the size, with no visible difference. A tester's character went from 1.08 MB to 0.27 MB. The importing app needs to support the extension (Blender, three.js, Babylon.js and most engines do), so it's off by default.
- **UVs are kept** from OBJ and glTF imports through sculpting, saving and export, including UDIM layouts, so a model can go back to texturing or baking with its UV layout intact. Remesh, dynamic detail, decimate and subdivision drop the UVs for now and tell you so. Undo brings them back.
- **Sharp Crease brush:** a crisp V-shaped fold along the stroke, for seams and panel lines. Alt raises a sharp ridge.
- **Sculpt layers made ahead of time** (Mesh > Strokes…, with Living strokes on): **+ New layer** and **Sculpt into** let you make a "Wrinkles" layer first, then sculpt on it.
- **Keep this level only** (Topology, and the Mesh menu): turns the subdivision level you see into the piece's mesh, so dynamic detail can work on it.
- **Python plugins say they're plugins:** they no longer turn on the local AI agent setting, and their undo steps are named after them.

## Fixed

- **With a pen on Windows, the brush cursor follows the pen while it hovers.** Before, it stayed where the pen last touched the tablet until you pressed again.
- **Clay Strips** has a round cursor like every other brush.
- **Dynamic detail after subdividing:** it's off on pieces with subdivision levels, because it would delete them. The Topology section now says so, with a **Keep this level only** button. Before, a status-bar line was the only sign.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. This beta adds two new project sections. Older versions open files that have them as follows:

| New section | What it saves | In older versions |
|---|---|---|
| `UVCO` | Texture coordinates (UVs) | Open without UVs |
| `TUBX` | A tube's Twist, Spin and Braid | The tube opens as ordinary mesh (it can't be reopened with Edit tube…) |
