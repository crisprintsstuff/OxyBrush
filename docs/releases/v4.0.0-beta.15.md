**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.15** puts **move, turn and scale in one transform gizmo**, and brings that gizmo to **Poly Edit**, where **Shift+drag extrudes** what's picked. Poly Edit also gains **soft selection**. You can **move several pieces together** and **merge a piece down** into the one below it. **Tube profiles** can have sharp and smooth corners point by point. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **One gizmo for move, turn and scale.** **T** shows all three at once:
  - arrows and squares move;
  - rings turn (the outer ring turns around the view);
  - cubes along each axis scale along it, and the circle round the centre scales evenly.

  T again hides it. The gizmo bar can still show **Move**, **Rotate** or **Scale** on its own.
- **The gizmo in Poly Edit** ([#272](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/272)): pick faces, edges or points and press T to move, turn and scale just them.
  - **Shift+drag** extrudes picked faces or border edges and moves the new ones; on a scale handle it insets faces.
  - The new geometry stays picked, so you can keep extruding.
- **Soft selection in Poly Edit** ([#275](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/275)): the gizmo also moves nearby points, less the further they are, for smooth bulges and bends. **Radius** and **Falloff** set how far it reaches and how it fades.
- **Poly Edit mode buttons** (Faces, Edges, Points, Auto) at the top of the brush shelf while Poly Edit is open.
- **Move several pieces together** ([#274](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/274)): **Ctrl/Cmd+click** pieces in the Pieces list, and the gizmo moves, turns and scales them all about their middle, as one undo step.
- **Merge down** ([#274](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/274)): join the active piece with the piece below it in the list, exactly as they are.
- **Sharp or smooth corners in tube profiles:** **double-click** a point in the Profile editor to make it a sharp corner, and again to round it. The **D-shape** preset now has a flat bottom.

## Fixed

- **Undo while editing a tube** undoes the tube's own edits, one step at a time, instead of the last change outside it ([#271](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/271)).
- **Mirror in the tube profile editor** adds and removes points on both sides ([#273](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/273)).
- **The wireframe shows low-poly primitives as quads again**, not as triangles ([#270](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/270)).
- **Poly Edit keeps the right pick after a gizmo move.** Before, the kept pick could land on other elements.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. This beta adds one new project section. Older versions open files that have it as follows:

| New section | What it saves | In older versions |
|---|---|---|
| `TPRC` | Which points of a tube's profile are sharp corners | The tube opens as ordinary geometry and can't be reopened with Edit tube |
