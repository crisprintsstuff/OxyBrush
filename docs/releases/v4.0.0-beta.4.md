**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.4** adds **Symmetrize** (mirror one half of a piece onto the other and weld the seam), **Split masked**, **Join** for combining pieces without remeshing, and custom **button and brush icon colors**. It also adds five new bundled plugins and fixes the retopology tool's **Tab+drag**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or come and talk about it on the new [OxyBrush Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Mesh > Symmetrize.** Makes a piece symmetric after the fact: it keeps one half, replaces the other half with its mirror image and welds the two along the middle, so you get one closed surface with no seam.
  - Choose **+X onto −X** or **−X onto +X** (and the same for Y and Z). The + side is where the axis gizmo's colored end points.
  - It mirrors about the piece's symmetry centre, the same one X symmetry uses.
  - Mask, vertex color and face groups are mirrored too. It's one undo step and removes subdivision levels.
- **Split masked** (Mask menu, Mesh menu, masking tools). Moves the masked part of a piece into a new piece and keeps the rest in the original. To split off the unmasked part instead, invert the mask first.
- **Join** (Pieces panel and menu). Combines the active piece and the other visible pieces into one piece, exactly as they are: nothing is remeshed or welded. Each piece keeps its own face groups, so you can still hide or select it.
- **Button and brush icon colors.** **View > Theme… > Colors** now lets you give buttons and other controls a color of your own, and color the brush icons to match your accent or any color you like (or keep the terracotta clay).
- **Five new bundled plugins** in the **Plugins** menu: **Group by Angle**, **Hide Faces by Angle**, **Color by Group**, **Fill Holes** and **Simplify Mesh**.

## Changed

- **Merge is now "Merge + remesh".** It always fused the visible pieces into a new surface with a voxel remesh, but the Pieces panel's button just said "Merge". Use **Join** to combine pieces without remeshing.

## Fixed

- **Retopology: Tab+drag pulls out a new quad.** Tab+drag on a border edge never worked in the app; it now does. If you drag so far that the new quad would leave the model, the status bar now says so.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged; this release doesn't change the project format.

## Known limitations

- **Symmetrize** welds only points that lie on the middle line. Parts that don't reach the middle (two separate eyes, say) are mirrored but not joined, and both halves get the same face groups.
- **Split masked** follows the existing triangles, so the cut edge is as jagged as the mesh is there.
- **Pen gestures** (from beta.3) and the **retopology pins** and **macOS icon fix** (from beta.2) still haven't been confirmed on real tablets or Macs.
- **Alphas carry over between projects** in one session: alphas from a project you closed stay in the list and are saved with the next project too.
- **Dynamic detail** is smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles and lags behind the brush around a million.
- The Blender bridge add-on still hasn't been run inside Blender.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
