**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.5** adds a **Deform window** with 25 whole-piece deformers, two new masking commands, **Mask border** and **Mask group borders**, and a new, easier-to-read interface font. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Deform window** (**Mesh > Deform…**). It changes the whole piece at once, or only its unmasked part:
  - **Shape:** move, rotate, scale, bend, S-bend, skew, S-skew, flatten (three kinds), twist, taper, squeeze, perspective and sag.
  - **Surface:** noise, inflate, inflate keep size and spherize.
  - **Smoothing:** smooth, polish (plain, or keeping detail, group edges or sharp edges) and relax.

  Each deformer is a bar with large **X / Y / Z** buttons for its axes. **Drag the bar** left or right to see the result live, and **let go** to apply it as one undo step; the bar springs back so you can drag again, and **Escape** cancels. **Repeat on this piece** and **Repeat on other pieces** apply the last one again, and **Fit to standard size** centres a piece and scales it to a standard size.
- **Mask border** and **Mask group borders** (**Mask** menu, [#170](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/170)): mask the edge of the visible surface (an open mesh's border, or the edge of what's left after hiding part of it), or the lines where face groups meet. Use **Mask > Grow** to widen them.

## Changed

- **New interface font: Inter.** It replaces the toolkit's thin default font and is sturdier and easier to read on dark panels. Text in the dark themes is also a little softer, and headings and highlighted text are no longer pure white.
- **Larger Mirror X / Y / Z buttons** in the toolbar and the Symmetry section, easier to hit with a mouse or a pen.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged; this release doesn't change the project format.

## Known limitations

- **Deform window:**
  - Only Twist has been tried in the running app; the other deformers are covered by automated tests. Please tell us if one behaves oddly.
  - The X / Y / Z choices reset when you restart.
  - Strong smoothing previews slowly on very dense meshes (about a tenth of a second per update at a million triangles).
- **The new font** has only been checked on Linux. Tell us how it looks on Windows and macOS.
- **Pen gestures** (from beta.3), and the **retopology pins** and **macOS icon fix** (from beta.2), still haven't been confirmed on real tablets or Macs.
- **Alphas carry over between projects** in one session: alphas from a project you closed stay in the list and are saved with the next project too.
- **Dynamic detail** is smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles and lags behind the brush around a million.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
