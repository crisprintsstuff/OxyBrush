**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.8** brings **Living strokes**, an option that keeps every stroke editable after you make it, and fills out **Poly Edit** with rounded bevels, bridges, creases and a live smooth subdivision preview. You can now start from a **human base mesh** or a **quad sphere**, and the voxel remesh handles **overlapping parts and holes**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app), or talk about it on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## New

- **Living strokes** (**Mesh > Strokes…**, first phase; off by default, turn it on in **Preferences > General** or with the button in the Strokes window). Each stroke stays editable after you make it. Pick one or several in the list and:
  - **fade** them with the Strength slider (0–150%, live while you drag),
  - **delete** them,
  - or **solo** one to see it without the strokes made after it.

  Later strokes keep what they did. Anything that changes the triangles (a remesh, subdivision, dynamic detail) keeps the surface and clears the list, and so does **Bake all**. Strokes aren't saved with the project yet.
- **More Poly Edit** (Mesh > Poly Edit):
  - **Faces:** **Bevel**, **Bridge** two faces with a tube (with rows and twist), **Equalize** the spacing of points.
  - **Edges:** **Bevel**, **Slide** along the edges beside them, **Spin** within their two faces, **Crease**.
  - **Points:** **Split face** between two points of a face, **Crease**.
  - **Rounded bevels:** **Bevel rows** (1–16) and a **Profile** from flat to round, for both face and edge bevels.
  - **Smooth:** a live **Preview** of smooth subdivision (up to 3 levels) while you edit. **Subdivide (smooth)** applies it; **Smooth and sculpt** applies it and closes Poly Edit. Creased edges stay sharp, or round off slowly at lower weights.
  - **Repeat (Y)** applies the last action to what's under the pointer. Bridge and Split face follow **Mirror across X** too.
- **Human base meshes** (File > New, the Welcome window and + Add): a realistic female and male body in quads, with the body parts as face groups. They come from Blender's Human Base Meshes bundle, released under CC0 by the Blender Foundation, and are credited in Help > About.
- **Quad and UV spheres** (+ Add > Sphere > **Kind**): **Quads**, now the default there, is an even ball of quads with no poles; **UV (rings)** has segments and rings; **Triangles** is the sphere it used to be.
- **Shell for the voxel remesh** (Topology > Rebuild > **Shell**): a wall of a chosen **Thickness**, outward, inward or both sides, instead of a solid. An open sheet gets that thickness; a closed model comes out hollow.
- **Keep the gizmo pivot in place** (gizmo bar > Pivot > **Follow the mask**, on by default): untick it and the pivot stays where it is while you mask and unmask.

## Changed

- **Remeshing a model with holes fills it** instead of making a thin wall around its surface. An open sheet isn't remeshed into a thin wall any more: the status bar suggests Shell.

## Fixed

- **Voxel remesh of overlapping parts** ([#174](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/174)): pieces whose parts overlap or fold through each other remeshed with holes and inner walls, or as a thin shell. The remesh now follows the outside only, and hollow models keep their cavity. Merge + remesh and the booleans get the same fix.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects from earlier betas open unchanged. Poly Edit creases are saved in a new project section; earlier betas open those files without the creases.
