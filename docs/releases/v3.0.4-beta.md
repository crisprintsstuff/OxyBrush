**OxyBrush 3.0.4-beta** gives the **transform gizmo** more operators: plane handles, snapping, typed values, pivot choices and mirror. **Plugins are now one click away** in a new **Plugins** menu, and OxyBrush comes with **built-in in-app plugins**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

## New

- **Transform gizmo operators** (turn on the gizmo with **T**):
  - **Plane handles:** the small squares between two axes move or scale along both at once.
  - **Snapping:** hold **Shift** while dragging a handle, or turn on **Snap** in the gizmo bar. Moves go in steps of 0.1, turns in 15° and scaling in 10 %; set the steps under **Pivot**.
  - **Typed values:** under the gizmo bar, type how far to move, turn or scale, then **Apply** (or Enter).
  - **Pivot:** put the pivot at the mask edge or centre, the bounding box centre or bottom, or the world origin. Set the axes to the world, or align them to the view.
  - **Operators:** **Mirror X / Y / Z**, **Put on the ground** (lowest point at Y = 0) and **Centre at origin**. These are also in the command palette.
  - Masked parts stay put, as when dragging, and each change is one undo step.
- **Plugins menu** in the menu bar:
  - Every in-app plugin has its commands there, plus its panel for settings.
  - Python scripts are listed too.
  - The menu also has **Install add-on…**, **Open plugins folder**, **Reload plugins** and **Manage plugins…**.
- **Built-in plugins**, ready to use with nothing to install:
  - **Displace:** inflate, deflate or roughen the surface.
  - **Mask by Height:** mask below or above a level.
  - **Color Ramp:** vertex color by height or by direction.
  - **Array:** copies of a piece in a row.
  - **Measure:** size and volume, with the bounding box drawn in the viewport.
- **Remove** for plugins you installed: **Settings > Plugins**, then click again to confirm.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet tried by testers:** everything above. The Blender bridge add-on still hasn't been run inside Blender; please tell us how it behaves.
- **Gizmo:**
  - Mirror works on the whole piece only (no mask).
  - Typed values and snap steps aren't remembered between sessions.
  - Shift-snapping was tested with simulated input, not yet with a real mouse or pen.
- **Plugins:**
  - Running a Python script switches on the local agent server, and needs Python 3 installed.
  - Add-ons are not signed.
- From 3.0.3-beta: primitive parameters can't be changed after Accept, and the Tube brush adds its tube as its own shell in the piece.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
