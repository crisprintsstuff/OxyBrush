**OxyBrush 3.0.0-beta** adds **dynamic detail**, so you can build a shape from a plain sphere, a **Curve Move** brush that bends parts along a curve, and strokes you can **adjust as curves** before applying them. It also fixes **symmetry on models away from the origin** and brings **tablet pressure to macOS and the Flatpak**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

## New

- **Dynamic detail** ([#81](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/81), first version): turn on **Add detail as you sculpt** in the Topology section. Sculpting brushes then add triangles where they need more detail and remove ones that have become too small, only where you sculpt. **Detail** and the brush size set how fine it gets: a smaller brush gives finer detail. Symmetry and masks are respected, and each stroke is one undo step.
- **Curve Move brush** ([#82](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/82)): draw a curve along a part (an arm, a tail), then drag its points; the part bends and stretches to follow, like a simple rig. **Size** sets how far from the curve the model follows. Masks protect what shouldn't move, symmetry moves the other side too, and **Enter** applies it as one undo step.
- **Curve strokes:** set **Stroke > Curve** and a stroke stays editable after you draw it. Drag its points to reshape it, click the curve to add a point, give a point its own size and strength, join the ends into a loop, then **Accept** (Enter). Or, while drawing a normal stroke, **hold still** for a moment to turn it into a curve.
- **Two-point pressure curve** (Preferences > Pen & tablet): drag two points to shape your pen's response, with an **S-curve** preset.
- **Symmetry centre per piece:** each piece mirrors about its own centre, chosen from its shape when it's imported or opened. **Recentre** and **World origin** buttons are in the Symmetry section, and turning on a mirror briefly shows its plane.
- **OxyCharacter round trip:** a document opened from OxyCharacter shows **Linked to OxyCharacter**; **Send back** saves it where OxyCharacter is watching.
- **Plugins can read the scene as data:** new local agent tools (also in the Python SDK) give the document, the camera and each piece's geometry in machine-readable form, and report changes as they happen. See [the agent guide](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/docs/mcp.md).

## Fixed

- **Symmetry on models away from the origin** ([#80](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/80)): mirroring worked about the world origin, so on a model that wasn't centred there (common with imports) the mirrored half of a stroke, including Grab, landed in empty space. Models already centred at the origin behave exactly as before.
- **macOS: tablet pressure** ([#70](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/70)): pen pressure from Wacom, Huion, XP-Pen and other tablets had no effect on macOS.
- **Linux Flatpak: tablet pressure** ([#81](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/81)): on Wayland the Flatpak couldn't switch to X11 for a tablet, so pressure was lost.
- **Side panels keep their width** ([#80](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/80)) as sections open and close.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects saved with 3.0.0-beta add two optional sections: each piece's **symmetry centre** and, for files from other programs, the original **vertex order**. Older versions open these projects and skip what they don't know.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet tried by testers:** dynamic detail, Curve Move and curve strokes; the macOS and Flatpak pressure fixes; the symmetry fix on real models. Please tell us how they behave on [#81](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/81), [#82](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/82), [#70](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/70) and [#80](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/80).
- **Dynamic detail** is smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles and falls behind the brush around a million; it works with Freehand strokes only, and is off on pieces with subdivision levels.
- **Curve Move:** changing the brush size with a curve open is slow on very dense meshes; curves aren't saved with the project.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
