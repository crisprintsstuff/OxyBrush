**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.24** makes OxySpheres a piece of their own that you can come back to and make new meshes from any time. It improves the Knife (cut a face out of a face, cuts over edges that reach your point), turns curve selection into a pen path, lets you paint the Poly Edit pick, and fixes Paint Groups border smoothing, Lighter inactive pieces and the macOS Plugins menu. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## OxySpheres

- **The spheres are a piece of their own, never used up.** **Make mesh** adds a new piece made from them and keeps the spheres as an OxySpheres piece (sphere icon in the Pieces list, hidden out of the mesh's way). Change them and make another mesh any time, however much you've sculpted the first. **Done** keeps the spheres without making a mesh. Click the OxySpheres piece, or use Pieces > Edit OxySpheres…, to open it again. [#453](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/453)
- **New spheres start on the symmetry plane**, not at the view's orbit point, which put them off centre once the view had moved. [#449](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/449)

## Poly Edit

- **Knife: cut a face out of a face.** Click points inside one face and the first point again: the loop becomes a new face, with a ring of faces joining it to the face's corners (a square loop in a quad is an inset). [#450](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/450)
- **Knife cuts over an edge reach the point you clicked.** A cut across a cap and down a side often stopped at the rim. Where a cut carries on past a click inside a face to its edge, the preview now draws that part dashed. [#452](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/452)
- **Paint the pick.** Ctrl/Cmd+drag picks every face, edge or point you pass over; started on a picked one, it takes them out. [#442](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/442)

## Masking and visibility

- **Curve selection as a pen path.** Drag from the first point and let go to apply (or click points and press Enter). Tap Alt while drawing to add a point the line bends through, and tap again quickly to make it a sharp corner. Hold Alt as you apply for the other side. [#446](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/446)
- **Ctrl+Shift+Alt+click hides a face group**, the opposite of Ctrl+Shift+click. [#435](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/435)

## Fixes and changes

- **Shift with Paint Groups smooths jagged group borders** by sliding the border's points into a smooth line, as Deform's "Polish, keep group edges" does. One undo step puts back the points and the groups. [#440](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/440)
- **Lighter inactive pieces show their lowest level.** Ordinary pieces were under the old triangle budget and didn't change, so the option seemed to do nothing; the budget setting is gone. [#451](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/451)
- **The brush ring shows while you resize from the quick menu**, as it does with S. [#433](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/433)
- **macOS: the Plugins menu lists your plugins from the start.** [#448](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/448)

## Project files

The `ARMS` section gets a version 2, which marks an OxySpheres piece. Projects from 4.0.0-beta.23.3 still open: a mesh saved with the spheres it was made from gets them back as an OxySpheres piece. 4.0.0-beta.23.3 and earlier open an OxySpheres piece as an ordinary mesh of the spheres. Keep a copy of your project before saving it in an earlier beta.
