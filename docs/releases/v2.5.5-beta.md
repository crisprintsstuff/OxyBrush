**OxyBrush 2.5.5-beta** is a fix-up release for 2.5.0: the **Insert Primitives** brush now shows the shape growing as you drag, the **cut tools** show their line and follow symmetry, a moved **gizmo pivot** stays put, and the **pen side button** works for navigation on Windows with Windows Ink. It also replaces names borrowed from other products with OxyBrush's own, makes the dark **Matte** theme readable again and keeps long **menus** on screen. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

**First launch:**

- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Fixed

- **Insert Primitives shows what you're inserting:** the primitive (and its symmetry copies) is drawn solid on the surface and grows as you drag, instead of appearing only when you release.
- **Cut tools show their line:** the Plane Clip, Line Split and Cut Away line, shaded side and direction arrow are visible while you drag. ("Hide circle during stroke" was hiding them; it now hides only the brush circle.)
- **Cut tools follow symmetry:** cuts are repeated for mirror and radial symmetry, as one undo step. A trim that would remove the whole piece is refused with a message.
- **Transform gizmo keeps a moved pivot:** after Alt-dragging the pivot and transforming, the gizmo stays with the geometry instead of snapping back.
- **Pen side button on Windows (Windows Ink)** ([#59](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/59), [#63](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/63)): holding the side button while touching the tablet no longer sculpts or masks. It acts as the right mouse button: choose the **Right-drag orbit** navigation preset (Settings > Keymap) to rotate with it, Ctrl to zoom and Alt to pan. A tap opens the quick menu.
- **Matte theme readability:** section headers, the Carve and Y/Z mirror toggles and the status bar were near-black with the orange accent; buttons are also lighter than the panels now.

## Changed

- **OxyBrush's own names:** the layout is **Workstation**, the theme **Matte**, the accent **Tangerine** (a new color), the keymap presets **Right-drag orbit** and **Single-key tools**, the material **Crimson clay**, Polyframe is **Wireframe + groups**, and the cut tools are **Plane Clip**, **Line Split** and **Cut Away**. The top bar always says Raise/Carve, Size and Strength. Your saved settings, keymaps and brush presets still load.
- **Menus fit on screen:** View is split into Camera, Shading, Brush cursor and Material submenus, and any menu taller than the window scrolls.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Known limitations

- The Windows Ink pen fix hasn't been tried on every tablet yet; if the side button still sculpts or masks, please comment on [#59](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/59).
- macOS is still untested on real Macs and not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
