## Changes in this release

### Realtime Matcap Preview on Hover
- Viewport shading dynamically updates as you hover over any material tile in the bottom shelf or in **View > Material**.
- Lets you preview how different matcaps reflect highlights, surface contours, and curvature across your sculpt without having to click to select.
- Reverts cleanly to the active material as soon as the mouse pointer leaves the thumbnail or popup.

### Dynamic Shift-Smooth Intensity Switching
- Holding `Shift` while sculpting immediately switches the UI strength slider to control the **Smooth** brush's intensity.
- Active across the top quick controls shelf, the vertical cursor quick menu (right-click / `Q`), and the brush settings panel.
- Allows sculptors to adjust smoothing strength on the fly without breaking their sculpting flow, mirroring ZBrush workflow.

### Quad Topology Preservation for OpenVDB Remeshing
- Resolved issue where OpenVDB voxel remeshing appeared as unstructured triangles in the viewport and in exported files.
- **Quad Wireframe Rendering:** Wireframe overlay (`Shift+W`) automatically detects quad diagonals and hides them, displaying a clean, uniform quad grid across the sculpt.
- **Quad OBJ Export:** OBJ exporter groups paired triangles sharing planar diagonals into native quad faces (`f a b c d`), ensuring full quad compatibility when importing into Blender, ZBrush, Maya, and 3ds Max.
- **Status Bar Quad Count:** Displays quad count metrics (e.g., `64.2k quads`) for meshes with quad geometry.
- **Settings Persistence:** The chosen `remesh_engine` (OpenVDB Sparse vs Regular Grid) is now remembered between sessions.
