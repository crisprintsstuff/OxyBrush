**OxyBrush 0.1.0-alpha** adds **macOS**, four new brushes, alphas, stroke control and masking/selection tools — the first half of Phase 3 on the [roadmap](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md). It's still an alpha: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

**First launch:** neither platform's package is signed by a paid developer certificate yet.
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg`, drag OxyBrush to Applications, and open it. macOS will refuse the first time; go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click the app and choose **Open**).
- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New in this release

- **macOS support** — one universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
- **New brushes:** Polish, Planar Trim, Topological Move (moves only the connected surface under the brush), Noise. 16 brushes in total.
- **Stroke control:** stabilizer for smooth lines, Line and Stamp stroke modes, spray, and a pressure curve.
- **Alphas:** shape each dab with an image — seven built-in procedural alphas (Noise, Bumps, Scales, Cracks, Stripes, Rings, Square) or your own grayscale PNG.
- **Masking & selection:** Ctrl+drag on empty space to mask a rectangle or lasso (Ctrl+Alt to unmask), Ctrl+click to blur; Ctrl+Shift+drag to show only a selection (Alt to hide it); mask cavities or peaks, mask a face group (Ctrl+M), blur/sharpen/grow/shrink the mask, grow/shrink the visible area. **L** switches rectangle/lasso.
- **For testers:** a log file, Help-menu links to report bugs with your system details pre-filled, Copy system info, Open log folder.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Known limitations

- **macOS is new and untested on real Macs** — we especially want reports on startup, the GPU and tablets.
- Double-clicking `.oxb` files doesn't open them on macOS yet (use File > Open).
- Pen pressure is still unverified on real tablets on every platform.
- Multiresolution and the 10M-triangle performance work are the rest of Phase 3.
- Decimating very dense meshes is slow; remesh resets mask and face groups; settings aren't remembered between sessions.
