## Changes in this release

**Mask Extract for Sculpting**
- Extract masked regions into a new piece with customizable extrusion thickness and boundary smoothing.
- Generates watertight 2-manifold solid shells with side rim walls and distinct face groups (front: 0, back: 1, rim: 2) or flat single-sided surface patches (`thickness = 0`).
- Laplacian boundary smoothing iterations remove stair-step jagged edges along the mask rim for clean, organic edges.
- Accessible via `Mask > Extract mask…`, the sidebar **Groups & mask** inspector panel with interactive controls, and Command Palette (`Mask: Extract`).
- Preserves preferences in `settings.txt`.

**Smooth Symmetry Blending (Issue #53)**
- Overlapping symmetry copies now blend using a $C^1$ smooth maximum partition-of-unity function.
- Eliminates the harsh midline crease or ridge line when pulling surfaces near or across the reflection plane, guaranteeing smooth continuous transitions without seam tearing or drift.

**Customizable Theme System**
- 6 base theme presets: `Graphite` (default dark neutral), `Charcoal` (deep OLED dark), `Slate` (cool studio blue-gray), `Warm Umber` (sculpting clay tone), `Studio Light` (clean bright neutral), and `High Contrast`.
- 7 accent color presets: `Terracotta` (signature OxyBrush clay), `Cyan` (electric blue), `Emerald` (calming mint), `Amber` (golden warm), `Crimson` (vivid ruby), `Violet` (studio purple), and `Custom` (full RGB color picker).
- Adjustable interface corner rounding styles: `Sharp` (0 px), `Rounded` (6 px default), and `Soft` (10 px).
- Dedicated "Theme & Appearance" submenu in Settings with live preview swatches, and quick switcher shortcuts in the Command Palette (`Ctrl+P`).
- Settings persist across sessions in `settings.txt`.

**High-Poly Transform Gizmo Performance & Configurable Subdivision Cap**
- Frame-rate pointer coalescing and direct parallel normal transformations prevent UI freezes when dragging transform gizmos on multi-million triangle meshes.
- Configurable subdivision triangle ceiling (`max_triangles` setting in Preferences and `settings.txt`, overridable via `OXYBRUSH_MAX_TRIANGLES`).

**Pixar Regularized Kelvinlet Brushes & Real-Time Wireframe Overlay**
- Elastodynamic volume-preserving brushes (Kelvin Grab, Kelvin Twist, Kelvin Scale) based on *de Goes & James (SIGGRAPH 2017)*.
- Hardware-accelerated wireframe overlay (`Shift+W`) with depth bias and opacity controls.
