**OxyBrush 3.0.1-beta** adds a **Tube tool** for horns, tentacles, tails and cables, **viewport lights and shadows**, **focal shift** with **S / U / I / O sliders under the pointer**, and **cylinder, cone and torus** pieces. It fixes the **jagged seams Grab could leave**, and a set of tester-reported bugs. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

## New

- **Tube tool:** **Pieces > + Add > Tube (draw)…**. Drag a path from the model or empty space and get a smooth tube along it. Drag its points to reshape it, **Shift+drag** a point to make the tube thicker or thinner there, click the line to add a point and **Alt+click** to remove one. The Tube window sets thickness, sides, rounded ends and a mirror copy across X; **Accept** adds it as a new piece.
- **Viewport lights and shadows:** **View > Shading > Light the viewport** shades the model with up to four lights (sun, point or spot) with soft shadows; **Lights…** sets them up, and numbered markers in the viewport move them. Off by default; the matcap stays the default look.
- **Focal shift:** in the brush settings and the quick menu, it moves the middle of the falloff, from firmer to softer. The cursor's inner ring follows it, and presets save it.
- **Sliders under the pointer:** **S** (size), **U** (strength), **I** (Paint strength) and **Shift+O** (focal shift) open one slider right where the pointer is. In the Single-key tools scheme, focal shift is **O** and orthographic moves to **P**. All can be changed in Settings > Keymap.
- **Cylinder, cone and torus pieces** under Pieces > + Add, with even triangles ready to sculpt.
- **Split hidden** (Mesh menu): moves the hidden faces into a new piece of their own.
- **Floor grid button** on the strip beside the viewport.

## Changed

- **History lists the newest step first**, with undone steps greyed at the top.
- **Ctrl+click on empty space inverts the mask** (with no mask, it masks everything), and a **Ctrl box around none of the model clears it**. Blur is in the Mask menu.

## Fixed

- **Grab left jagged seams**, especially with X symmetry while pulling along the normal: a step where the mirrored brush's reach began, and steep walls torn by **Front faces only**. That setting now fades vertices out as the surface turns away, for every brush.
- **Windows: app icon** in the window and taskbar.
- **Open log folder** opened a web browser on Windows instead of Explorer.
- **Tab** kept moving through the menus instead of showing the panels again.
- **Brush shelf icons** were small and out of line with the current brush, alpha and material tiles.
- The README and website now count **19 sculpting brushes plus 6 tools**.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

Unchanged from 3.0.0-beta: projects open in either version.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet tried by testers:** the tube tool, lights and shadows, focal shift and the pointer sliders, and the Grab fix on real models. Please tell us how they behave.
- **Tube tool:** points move flat to the view (they don't snap to the surface); the tube is a separate piece until you **Merge**; round profile only; it can't be edited after Accept.
- **Lights:** each shadow-casting light redraws the model once more per frame; turn shadows off for lights that don't need them on very dense meshes.
- **Dynamic detail** is smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles and falls behind the brush around a million; Freehand strokes only.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
