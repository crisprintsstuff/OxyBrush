**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.3** brings **one Theme window** for everything about how OxyBrush looks, a **slider thickness** option, and **pen gestures**: with a pen you can now scroll panels by dragging them, and hold the pen still to open menus. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

**First launch:**
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg`, drag OxyBrush to Applications, and open it from there. If macOS shows an untrusted developer warning, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **One Theme window.** **View > Theme…** (also in the command palette and in **Preferences > Interface**) has three tabs:
  - **Themes:** the nine themes, each drawn in its own colors, plus three ready-made looks (Classic, Workstation, Midnight).
  - **Colors:** the accent color and the viewport background.
  - **Size & shape:** interface scale, corner style and slider thickness.

  **Reset everything** goes back to the default look. This replaces the long Preferences > Appearance tab and View's Background menu; Preferences > Appearance is now **Preferences > Interface**.
- **Slider thickness.** Choose **Thin**, **Normal** or **Thick** slider bars under **Theme > Size & shape**. Only the bars change, not the rest of the interface. Thick bars are easier to see and to hit with a pen.
- **Pen gestures.** Several testers couldn't scroll panels with a pen. Now, once OxyBrush has seen pen pressure:
  - **Drag to scroll:** dragging over the brush list, the right panel or a window scrolls it. Sliders and other controls still take the drag as before.
  - **Press and hold:** holding the pen still for about a second works as a right-click. It opens menus and shows a control's tooltip.
  - **Side buttons:** holding a side button and dragging over a panel scrolls it too.
  - **Pen in panels** in **Preferences > Pen & tablet** switches this between pen only (the default), always (also for a mouse) and off.
- **Pen side buttons navigation preset.** In **Preferences > Keymap**, this preset orbits with the side button that acts as a right-click (Shift pans, Ctrl zooms) and pans with the one that acts as a middle-click.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Project files

Projects open unchanged; this release doesn't change the project format. Settings files from earlier versions load unchanged too. Older versions ignore the two new settings (slider thickness and pen in panels).

## Known limitations

- **Pen gestures haven't been tried on a real tablet yet.** They were tested with simulated pen input only. Please tell us how they behave on your tablet and operating system, and whether the 1-second hold feels right. If a gesture gets in the way, set **Pen in panels** to **Off**.
- Looks no longer change your shortcuts, navigation or material; set those in the Keymap and the material picker.
- **Alphas carry over between projects** in one session: alphas from a project you closed stay in the list and are saved with the next project too.
- **Import scaling** goes by size alone, so an import that really is meant to be 10× the scene gets scaled too: press Undo once to keep its original size.
- The box and lasso **hide/show** tools don't follow symmetry yet (masking does).
- **Dynamic detail** is smooth up to a few hundred thousand triangles and lags behind the brush around a million.
- The Blender bridge add-on still hasn't been run inside Blender.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
