**OxyBrush 2.5.7-beta** brings a **live preview for mask extract**, a **floor grid** that follows your model, **pen pressure settings**, and a fix so **quitting on macOS asks to save**. It's still a beta: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

**First launch:**
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg`, drag OxyBrush to Applications, and open it. If macOS shows an untrusted developer warning, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New

- **Floor grid:** a grid on the floor under the model, with a bolder line every ten cells, fading out with distance. It stays at the bottom of the visible pieces and its spacing follows the model's size, so it moves and rescales as you sculpt. Turn it off in **View > Shading > Floor grid**.
- **Pen & tablet settings** ([#70](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/70)): **Preferences > Pen & tablet** has a large pressure curve with a live dot for your pen, **Soft / Linear / Firm** presets, the curve shape, **Lightest touch** (a minimum strength for light strokes) and **Full pressure at** (how hard you press for full strength). Remembered between sessions. Tip: keep your tablet driver's own curve straight so the two don't add up.

## Changed

- **Mask extract previews first:** **Extract mask** now shows the shell as a preview. Adjust **Thickness** and **Smooth edge** and watch it update, then **Accept** (Enter) to add it as a new piece, or **Cancel** (Esc) to leave the scene unchanged.

## Fixed

- **macOS: quitting asks to save** ([#69](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/69)): Cmd+Q, Quit in the Dock and logging out closed OxyBrush without asking to save unsaved changes. They now ask, like closing the window does, and Cancel keeps OxyBrush open.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

## Known limitations

- The macOS Quit fix and the pen pressure settings haven't been tried on a real Mac or a Huion tablet yet; please tell us on [#69](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/69) and [#70](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/70) how they behave.
- On very dense meshes the mask extract preview may update slowly while you drag its sliders.
- macOS is not notarized; pen pressure and touch gestures are untested on many tablets.
- Vertex color isn't exported yet; remesh, decimate and merge remove it.
