**OxyBrush 4.0.0-beta.21.4** makes placed recipes editable on the surface, rounds out symmetric Grab-family pulls, and ships an installable Blender bridge extension. Please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) or share feedback on [Discord](https://discord.gg/Vfm7Km7xvG).

## What’s new

- **Recipe Box.** The Parts Bin has a new name. Use **Move on surface** to reposition a placed recipe and adjust its twist and size. Mirrored pairs stay linked during edits until you unlink them. You can save the changes back to the box or save a new recipe. Placement now follows the pointer, and placed recipes keep their editable stroke groups across save and reopen. [#392](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/392) · [#393](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/393)
- **New tabs inherit the current material.** Choose a default or a specific material under **Preferences > General > New tabs start with**. [#394](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/394)
- **Blender bridge ZIP.** Install the OBJ bridge from disk in Blender and set the path to OxyBrush in the extension preferences. [Installation guide](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/tree/main/integrations/blender) · [#396](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/396)

## Fixes

- **Rounded pulls with mirror symmetry.** Grab, Topological Move, Kelvin Grab, and Snake Hook produce rounded shapes near the mirror plane, while the point under the cursor follows the drag. [#395](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/395)
- **Pieces list clicks.** Visibility, double-click rename, and Alt-click isolate respond again. [#391](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/pull/391)

## Project files

This release reads placed recipes from beta.21 and beta.21.2. It writes version 2 of the `PART` section to preserve placed recipes across save and reopen. Keep a copy of your original project if you plan to reopen it in an earlier beta.
