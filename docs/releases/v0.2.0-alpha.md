**OxyBrush 0.2.0-alpha** adds **subdivision levels** and a much friendlier, rearrangeable interface with **Standard, Compact and Touch layouts**. It's still an alpha: please [report issues](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) (or use **Help > Report a bug…** in the app).

**First launch:** the packages aren't signed with a paid developer certificate yet.
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg`, drag OxyBrush to Applications, and open it. macOS refuses the first time; go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** (on macOS 14 or older, right-click the app and choose **Open**).
- **Windows:** SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## New in this release

- **Subdivision levels:** **Ctrl+D** adds a level, **D** / **Shift+D** move up and down. Sculpt big shapes on a low level and fine detail on a high one; broad changes carry the detail along. Levels are saved in projects and covered by undo.
- **Welcome window** on first start: pick a starting shape, open or import, learn the basics and choose a layout.
- **Layouts:** **Standard** (mouse and keyboard), **Compact** (small screens) and **Touch friendly** (large buttons, brush tiles, on-screen Smooth / Mask / Invert instead of modifier keys). Settings > Layout.
- **Arrange it your way:** brushes on the left, **along the bottom** or on the right; settings panel on either side; **size, strength and Raise/Carve centered over the viewport** (top or bottom) or in the toolbar. **Drag brushes** to reorder them; number keys follow your order. Settings > Arrange panels, or right-click the shelf.
- **Previews:** brush, alpha and material previews with thumbnail pickers; every brush has an icon showing what it does.
- **Viewport:** gradient background, a clickable axis gizmo, and a strip of drag-to-orbit / pan / zoom buttons (handy with a pen).
- **Settings are remembered** between sessions: layout, arrangement, brush order, interface scale, theme, material and autosave interval.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/crisprintsstuff/OxyBrush/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) for details.

**Changed shortcut:** **D** used to subdivide in place; it now moves up a subdivision level. The one-off subdivide is **Mesh > Subdivide (single mesh)**.

## Known limitations

- Adding or switching to a very dense subdivision level (~5M triangles) pauses the app for a few seconds.
- The Touch layout has no multi-touch gestures yet (pinch zoom, two-finger orbit) and hasn't been tried on real touchscreens or tablets; reports very welcome.
- macOS is still untested on real Macs, and double-clicking `.oxb` files doesn't open them there yet (use File > Open).
- Pen pressure is still unverified on real tablets on every platform.
- Brush settings (radius, strength, falloff) aren't remembered between sessions yet.
